Donauwörth is a colorful Bavarian town on Germany's Romantic Road, set where the Wörnitz flows into the Danube. Its historic center is built around elegant Reichsstraße, with pastel façades, Rieder Tor, the town hall and Münster Unserer Lieben Frau among its best-known sights.

Visitors can stay around Reichsstraße for the most atmospheric and walkable location, near the railway station for convenient regional connections, or closer to the Danube for quieter surroundings and easy access to riverside paths.

Donauwörth travel tips

Walk Along Reichsstraße

Begin at Rieder Tor and follow Reichsstraße through the Old Town to admire colorful historic façades, the town hall and Münster Unserer Lieben Frau. Morning and early evening are pleasant times for a quieter walk.

Explore the Danube & Wörnitz

Follow the riverside paths near the meeting point of the Danube and Wörnitz. The area is well suited to walking and cycling, while the Old Town remains close enough to combine both in a relaxed day.

Plan Your Romantic Road Stop

Donauwörth sits between Nördlingen and Augsburg, making it a useful base for continuing along the Romantic Road. Check hotel parking before arrival and allow extra time if you plan to travel by regional train.

Donauwörth FAQ

Is Donauwörth worth visiting?

Yes. Donauwörth is a colorful Romantic Road town at the meeting point of the Danube and Wörnitz rivers, known for Reichsstraße, Rieder Tor and its historic Old Town.

Where is the best place to stay in Donauwörth?

The Old Town around Reichsstraße is the most convenient choice for first-time visitors. The station area suits rail travelers, while accommodation near the Danube may provide a quieter riverside setting.

How many days do you need in Donauwörth?

One full day covers the main Old Town highlights, while staying one or two nights gives you more time for riverside walks and nearby Romantic Road towns.

Can you walk around Donauwörth?

Yes. The historic center is compact and easy to explore on foot. Comfortable shoes are useful on older streets, and the walk between the railway station and the Old Town takes a little longer.
