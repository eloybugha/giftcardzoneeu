Offenbach am Main is located directly east of Frankfurt in the German state of Hesse. The city combines a multicultural center, museums, riverside neighborhoods, business districts and convenient regional transport.

Visitors can stay in the city center for shopping and restaurants, near the Main River for modern surroundings and walking routes, or around Kaiserlei and the railway stations for easy connections to Frankfurt.

Offenbach am Main FAQ

Is Offenbach am Main a good place to stay?

Yes. Offenbach offers convenient transport, restaurants, cultural attractions, riverside areas and easy access to Frankfurt.

Where is the best area to stay in Offenbach?

The city center is convenient for shopping, restaurants and public transport. Kaiserlei is suitable for business travelers and visitors who want quick connections to Frankfurt.

Is Offenbach close to Frankfurt?

Yes. Offenbach is located directly east of Frankfurt and has frequent S-Bahn, regional rail, bus and road connections.
