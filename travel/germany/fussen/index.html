Find your ideal stay in Füssen

Füssen is one of Bavaria's most scenic destinations and the southern end of Germany's Romantic Road. Its compact Old Town, Alpine surroundings and proximity to Neuschwanstein Castle make it an excellent base for sightseeing.

  • Best for first visits: Füssen Old Town
  • Best for transport: Near Füssen Station
  • Best for castles: Hohenschwangau
  • Best for nature: Lech River and nearby lakes

Why stay in Füssen?

Füssen offers more than a convenient gateway to Neuschwanstein Castle. The town has its own attractive historic center, museums, riverside walks and impressive Alpine views. Staying overnight also makes it easier to explore the castles before or after the busiest daytime visiting hours.

Füssen travel tips

Book Castle Tickets Early

Neuschwanstein is one of Germany's most visited attractions. Reserve dated castle tickets in advance when possible, particularly during summer, weekends and holiday periods.

Use Füssen as Your Base

Central Füssen provides restaurants, shops and convenient transport. Local buses connect the station with Hohenschwangau, making it possible to visit the castles without staying directly beside them.

Allow Time for Nature

Beyond the castles, explore the Lech River, Lechfall and nearby lakes. Comfortable walking shoes and layers are useful because weather can change quickly close to the Alps.

Füssen FAQ

Is Füssen worth visiting?

Yes. Füssen combines a colorful medieval Old Town, Alpine scenery, the Lech River and convenient access to Neuschwanstein and Hohenschwangau castles.

Where is the best place to stay in Füssen?

Füssen Old Town is the best choice for first-time visitors because it is close to restaurants, shops, attractions and the train station. Hohenschwangau is more convenient for travelers focused on the castles.

How many days do you need in Füssen?

Two days are suitable for exploring Füssen and visiting Neuschwanstein Castle. Three days allow additional time for Hohenschwangau, nearby lakes and scenic Alpine walks.

How do you get from Füssen to Neuschwanstein Castle?

Local buses connect Füssen station with Hohenschwangau village. From the village, visitors can walk or use available seasonal transport toward the castle area.
