Package booking questions

Can I book a flight and hotel together?

Search with your departure city and dates. Check the hotel address, arrival airport and transfer cost before booking a bundle.

What if the quote is per person?

Use the provider’s checkout total for your whole party. If the hotel quote is nightly, enter the total for every room and night, including mandatory charges.

Can I compare a rail trip with a flight package?

Yes. Enter the return rail fare under separate transport. Add any onward journeys, then compare both cost and travel time.
