Schongau is a picturesque Bavarian town on Germany's Romantic Road, set beside the fast-flowing Lech River. Its historic center is built around Marienplatz, with the town hall, Ballenhaus, Stadtpfarrkirche Mariä Himmelfahrt and preserved town wall among its best-known sights.

Visitors can stay around Marienplatz for the most atmospheric and walkable location, near the railway station for convenient regional connections, or closer to the Lech for river views and easy access to waterfront paths.

Schongau travel tips

Explore Marienplatz

Begin at Marienplatz and walk through the Old Town to admire colorful façades, the historic town hall, Ballenhaus and nearby churches. Morning and early evening are pleasant times for a quieter walk.

Walk Beside the Lech

Follow local paths toward the Lech for peaceful water and countryside views. The elevated Old Town and river surroundings can be combined in a relaxed day of walking or cycling.

Plan Your Romantic Road Stop

Schongau sits south of Augsburg, making it a useful base for continuing along the Romantic Road toward Peiting, Rottenbuch and Füssen. Check hotel parking before arrival, especially when staying inside the Old Town.

Schongau FAQ

Is Schongau worth visiting?

Yes. Schongau is a picturesque Bavarian town on the Romantic Road, known for its historic Old Town, Marienplatz, Ballenhaus, preserved city wall and setting above the Lech valley.

Where is the best place to stay in Schongau?

The Old Town around Marienplatz is the most convenient choice for first-time visitors. The station area suits rail travelers, while accommodation near the Lech may provide a scenic riverside setting.

How many days do you need in Schongau?

One full day covers the main Old Town highlights, while staying one or two nights gives you more time for riverside walks and nearby Romantic Road towns.

Can you walk around Schongau?

Yes. The historic center is compact and easy to explore on foot. Comfortable shoes are useful on older streets and on the slopes between the river and the upper parts of the Old Town.
