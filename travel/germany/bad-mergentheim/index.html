Bad Mergentheim is a historic spa town in Baden-Württemberg's scenic Tauber Valley. Its compact center is known for the market square, attractive historic buildings, local restaurants, the Kurpark and the Deutschordensschloss castle complex.

Visitors can stay in the Old Town for sightseeing and dining, beside the Kurpark for wellness and quiet surroundings, near Deutschordensschloss for history, or close to the station for practical regional train connections.

Bad Mergentheim FAQ

Is Bad Mergentheim worth visiting?

Yes. Bad Mergentheim offers a historic Old Town, the Kurpark, spa facilities, Deutschordensschloss and scenic Tauber Valley surroundings.

Where is the best place to stay in Bad Mergentheim?

The Old Town is convenient for historic attractions, restaurants, shops and exploring the compact center on foot.

Is Bad Mergentheim convenient for train travel?

Yes. Bad Mergentheim station is useful for regional connections and for visitors exploring destinations around the Tauber Valley.

Is Bad Mergentheim good for cycling?

Yes. The town sits on popular Tauber Valley cycling routes. Check the route, weather and bicycle facilities at your accommodation before traveling.
