Rheydt is a separate centre

Mönchengladbach has two main centres: the historic Mönchengladbach centre and Rheydt, with its market square. Do not treat Mönchengladbach Hauptbahnhof and Rheydt Hauptbahnhof as interchangeable when choosing a hotel. Compare the route from the station actually used by your train.

If visiting Museum Schloss Rheydt, check a separate route to the museum rather than assuming that an address described as “Rheydt” is within a short walk of it.

Football travel

Getting to Borussia-Park on match day

NEW, the local transport operator, lists match-day stadium shuttles from both Mönchengladbach Hauptbahnhof and Rheydt Hauptbahnhof. This makes a station-area hotel another option to compare with accommodation near the stadium.

Check the operator's current departure points, operating times, return arrangements and ticket rules for your fixture. Do not assume match-day shuttles operate for stadium tours, concerts or ordinary days.

For an evening fixture, check your final train departure before booking. Leave room in your plan for queues after the match, and confirm late check-in if you will reach the hotel afterward.
