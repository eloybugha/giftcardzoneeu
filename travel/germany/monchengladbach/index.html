Quick decision guide

Which area fits your trip?

Start with the journey you expect to make most often. For a short stay, a hotel near your evening plans can be more convenient than one close to a place you will visit only once.

What each area is like for a stay

City centre and Abteiberg

Start here if your weekend revolves around Museum Abteiberg, Alter Markt and dinner in the Old Town. Compare the hotel’s walking route to the square. If you prefer a quieter room, check recent reviews for street noise.

Arriving by train Mönchengladbach Hbf

Near Mönchengladbach Hauptbahnhof

A station-area hotel is a practical choice when you have luggage or an early departure. Check the walking route from the entrance to reception and confirm late check-in if your train arrives in the evening.

Rheydt

Look here if you are meeting someone in Rheydt or your train uses Rheydt Hauptbahnhof. For a castle visit, map the journey to Schloss Rheydt separately. A hotel near the station can still require another journey to the castle.

Near Borussia-Park

For a match or concert, compare the route from the stadium exit to your hotel. If you also want an evening in the Old Town, compare a central hotel and check the return transport for your event date.

Questions about staying in Mönchengladbach

Where should I stay for a first sightseeing weekend?

The city centre is a good starting point if Museum Abteiberg, Alter Markt and Old Town restaurants are on your list. Search around those places on the hotel map to see which rooms suit your walking plans.

Are Mönchengladbach Hbf and Rheydt Hbf the same station?

They are separate stations. Match the full station name on your ticket to the map before choosing a hotel, especially for a late arrival or early train.

Should I stay near Borussia-Park for a match?

A stadium-area hotel makes sense if the match is your main activity and the return route works for you. For a longer city weekend, a central hotel may suit the rest of your stay better. Check matchday transport for your specific event.
