Creglingen is a peaceful historic town in Baden-Württemberg's scenic Tauber Valley. It lies along Germany's Romantic Road and is known for its traditional town center, surrounding countryside and the Herrgottskirche, which contains an important altar by sculptor Tilman Riemenschneider.

Visitors can stay around Creglingen Old Town for convenient access to local restaurants and historic streets, near the Herrgottskirche for culture and sightseeing, or in the Tauber Valley countryside for cycling, hiking and a relaxed rural atmosphere.

Creglingen travel tips

Explore the Old Town

Walk through Creglingen's compact historic center and discover its traditional streets, local cafés, restaurants and peaceful small-town atmosphere.

Visit Herrgottskirche

Allow time to visit the Herrgottskirche and see the famous carved altar associated with German sculptor Tilman Riemenschneider. Check current opening hours before visiting.

Discover the Tauber Valley

Explore the surrounding valley by bicycle, car or on foot. Check weather, route conditions and bicycle storage at your accommodation before traveling.

Creglingen FAQ

Is Creglingen worth visiting?

Yes. Creglingen offers a peaceful historic center, beautiful Tauber Valley scenery, Romantic Road experiences and the famous Herrgottskirche with its Riemenschneider altar.

Where is the best place to stay in Creglingen?

The historic Old Town is convenient for first-time visitors, while the surrounding Tauber Valley countryside is ideal for cycling, walking and quieter stays.

Is Creglingen on the Romantic Road?

Yes. Creglingen is located along Germany's Romantic Road and works well as a peaceful base between Weikersheim and Rothenburg ob der Tauber.

Is Creglingen good for cycling and hiking?

Yes. The surrounding Tauber Valley offers scenic cycling and walking routes. Check current weather, route conditions and bicycle facilities before traveling.
