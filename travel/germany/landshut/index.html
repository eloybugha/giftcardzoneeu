Landshut is a historic Bavarian city known for its colorful Old Town, Gothic architecture, Trausnitz Castle and attractive setting beside the Isar River. Its compact center combines major landmarks, traditional streets, shops and restaurants.

Visitors can stay in the Old Town for sightseeing and dining, near Trausnitz Castle for historic scenery and views, or close to the station for convenient regional rail connections.
