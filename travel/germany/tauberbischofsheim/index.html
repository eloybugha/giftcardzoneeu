Tauberbischofsheim is a historic town in Baden-Württemberg's scenic Tauber Valley. Its compact center is known for half-timbered buildings, the market square, local restaurants and the landmark Kurmainz Castle complex.

Visitors can stay in the Old Town for sightseeing and dining, near Kurmainz Castle for historic surroundings, by the Tauber for scenery, or close to the station for practical regional train connections.
