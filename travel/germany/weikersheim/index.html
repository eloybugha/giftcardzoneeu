Weikersheim is a charming town in Baden-Württemberg's scenic Tauber Valley. Its compact center is known for the Renaissance palace, impressive Baroque gardens, historic Market Square, half-timbered buildings and relaxed Franconian atmosphere.

Visitors can stay around the Old Town and Market Square for sightseeing and dining, near Weikersheim Palace for architecture and gardens, or close to the station for practical regional train connections.

Weikersheim FAQ

Is Weikersheim worth visiting?

Yes. Weikersheim offers a historic Old Town, Weikersheim Palace, Baroque gardens, the historic Market Square and scenic Tauber Valley surroundings.

Where is the best place to stay in Weikersheim?

The Old Town and Market Square are convenient for restaurants, historic architecture, the palace and exploring the compact center on foot.

Is Weikersheim convenient for train travel?

Yes. Weikersheim station is useful for regional connections and for visitors traveling toward Bad Mergentheim and other destinations around the Tauber Valley.

Is Weikersheim good for cycling?

Yes. The town sits on popular Tauber Valley cycling routes. Check the route, weather and bicycle facilities at your accommodation before traveling.
