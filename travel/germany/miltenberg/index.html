Miltenberg is a picturesque Bavarian town beside the Main River, between the Spessart and Odenwald landscapes. Its compact historic center is known for half-timbered buildings, traditional streets, local restaurants and the hilltop Mildenburg Castle.

Visitors can stay in the Old Town for sightseeing and dining, near the castle for historic surroundings and elevated views, or close to the station for practical regional train connections.

Miltenberg travel tips

Explore the Old Town

Walk through historic lanes, admire half-timbered houses and visit the traditional market square known as Schnatterloch.

Visit Mildenburg Castle

Climb toward the historic castle to explore its museum collections and enjoy elevated views across the town and Main valley.

Walk beside the Main

Enjoy riverside paths, cycling routes and relaxing views before exploring nearby Bürgstadt, Kleinheubach and other Main River destinations.

Miltenberg FAQ

Is Miltenberg worth visiting?

Yes. Miltenberg offers a picturesque medieval Old Town, half-timbered houses, Mildenburg Castle, museums and Main River scenery.
