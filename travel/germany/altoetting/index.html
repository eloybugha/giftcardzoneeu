Find your ideal stay in Altötting

Altötting is one of Bavaria's most important pilgrimage towns, known for the Chapel of Grace, Kapellplatz and its collection of historic churches. Its compact center is an appealing base for religious heritage, museums and relaxed local dining.

  • Best for rail travelers: Station area
  • Best for quiet stays: Neuötting
  • Best for pilgrimage visits: Basilica of St. Anna area

Kapellplatz and Town Center

Kapellplatz is the most convenient choice for first-time visitors and pilgrims. The Chapel of Grace, St. Magdalena Church, museums, cafés and shops are all nearby, making the historic center easy to explore comfortably on foot.

Why stay in Altötting?

Altötting combines major Bavarian pilgrimage heritage with a compact, walkable center. Altötting travel tips

Book Peak-Season Stays Early

Altötting becomes especially busy during major pilgrimages, religious festivals, summer weekends and Christmas events. Compare availability early if you want accommodation within walking distance of Kapellplatz.

Choose a Walkable Central Base

Kapellplatz and the main pilgrimage sights are close together, so a central hotel makes sightseeing easy without a car. Check current train and bus connections before arrival, especially when traveling with luggage.

Combine Culture and Nature

Beyond the pilgrimage center, allow time for Neuötting, nearby cycling routes and destinations across the Inn-Salzach region. Comfortable shoes, rain protection and layered clothing are useful throughout the year.

Altötting FAQ

Is Altötting worth visiting?

Yes. Altötting is one of Bavaria's best-known pilgrimage towns, centered on Kapellplatz and the historic Chapel of Grace.

Where is the best place to stay in Altötting?

Kapellplatz and the town center are best for first-time visitors because they are close to churches, museums, restaurants and shops. The station area suits rail travelers, while Neuötting offers a quieter alternative.

How many days do you need in Altötting?

One or two days are enough for Kapellplatz, the main pilgrimage sights and local museums. A longer stay allows trips to Neuötting, Burghausen and the wider region.

How do you get to Altötting?

Regional trains serve Altötting railway station with connections across southeastern Bavaria. The station is within walking distance of the center, and local buses serve nearby communities.
