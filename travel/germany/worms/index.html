Worms is one of Germany’s historically important Rhine cities. It is known for Worms Cathedral, medieval legends, religious heritage, museums and its location beside the Rhine River in Rhineland-Palatinate.

Visitors can stay near the Old Town for restaurants and sightseeing, close to the cathedral for historic attractions, or near Worms Central Station for convenient regional train connections.

Quick Worms guide

  • Best for first visits: Old Town
  • Best for landmarks: Cathedral area
  • Best for transport: Central Station
  • Best for scenery: Rhine River side
