Landsberg am Lech is a picturesque Bavarian town on Germany's Romantic Road, set beside the fast-flowing Lech River. Its historic center is built around colorful Hauptplatz, with the town hall, Schmalzturm, Bayertor and riverside Lechwehr among its best-known sights.

Visitors can stay around Hauptplatz for the most atmospheric and walkable location, near the railway station for convenient regional connections, or closer to the Lech for river views and easy access to waterfront paths.

Landsberg am Lech travel tips

Explore Hauptplatz

Begin at Hauptplatz and walk through the Old Town to admire colorful façades, the historic town hall, Schmalzturm and nearby churches. Morning and early evening are pleasant times for a quieter walk.

Walk Beside the Lech

Follow the riverside paths and stop near the Lechwehr for classic views of the water and historic buildings. The Old Town is close enough to combine with a relaxed riverside walk in the same day.

Plan Your Romantic Road Stop

Landsberg am Lech sits south of Augsburg, making it a useful base for continuing along the Romantic Road toward Schongau and the Alpine foothills. Check hotel parking before arrival, especially when staying inside the Old Town.

Landsberg am Lech FAQ

Is Landsberg am Lech worth visiting?

Yes. Landsberg am Lech is a picturesque Bavarian town on the Romantic Road, known for its historic Old Town, colorful Hauptplatz, Bayertor and the Lech riverfront.

Where is the best place to stay in Landsberg am Lech?

The Old Town around Hauptplatz is the most convenient choice for first-time visitors. The station area suits rail travelers, while accommodation near the Lech may provide a scenic riverside setting.

How many days do you need in Landsberg am Lech?

One full day covers the main Old Town highlights, while staying one or two nights gives you more time for riverside walks and nearby Romantic Road towns.

Can you walk around Landsberg am Lech?

Yes. The historic center is compact and easy to explore on foot. Comfortable shoes are useful on older streets and on the slopes between the river and the upper parts of the Old Town.
