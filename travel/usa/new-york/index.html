Where to Stay in New York for a First Visit

CENTRAL SIGHTSEEING

Midtown Manhattan

Start with Midtown if Broadway, Times Square and central Manhattan are the focus of your trip. Compare hotels around Midtown East, Midtown West and Bryant Park instead of assuming every Midtown address offers the same experience.

Check recent reviews for street noise, elevator waits and room size. A room away from busy roads may suit you better than a prominent view.

PARKS AND NEIGHBORHOOD WALKS

Upper West Side

Consider the Upper West Side when Central Park, the American Museum of Natural History and nearby neighborhood walks feature in your plans. Compare the hotel's location against your chosen subway stations.

For families, confirm bedding, maximum occupancy and whether the room has enough space for luggage or a stroller.

FOOD AND CULTURE

Chelsea and Flatiron

These areas are worth comparing for a trip that includes restaurants, galleries and walks around this part of Manhattan. Chelsea can be convenient for visiting the High Line, depending on the hotel address.

Look at the route to your evening activities and airport connection, alongside the nightly rate.

DOWNTOWN ITINERARY

Lower Manhattan

Consider Lower Manhattan if your itinerary centers on the Financial District, the 9/11 Memorial and downtown waterfront areas. Check the exact hotel location rather than relying on a broad “downtown” label.

Compare transport connections for any planned visits to Midtown, Central Park or Brooklyn.

Should You Stay Outside Manhattan?

Long Island City, Queens

Include Long Island City in your hotel search if you are open to staying across the East River. Compare the total cost with Manhattan options for the same dates and room requirements.

Check walking distance to a station, transfers and late-evening routes. A hotel described as being in Long Island City is not necessarily beside the waterfront or a convenient station.

Brooklyn

Brooklyn may suit a trip with activities in Williamsburg, Downtown Brooklyn or other specific neighborhoods. Choose a neighborhood first, then compare individual hotels.

Do not assume Brooklyn will always be cheaper. Calculate the journeys to your planned Manhattan destinations before booking.

How to Compare New York Hotel Costs

  • Use the same dates and guest count: compare equivalent room types and bedding.
  • Check the final total: review taxes and any mandatory property charges shown before payment.
  • Read cancellation terms: check the deadline, applicable time zone and refund conditions.
  • Confirm room details: look at room size, bathroom type, air conditioning and accessibility needs.
  • Check recent reviews: pay attention to noise, cleanliness and construction reports.
  • Include transport: a lower room rate may involve more journeys or transfers.
A useful comparison

For a short trip focused on Midtown, a central hotel may justify a higher rate by reducing travel time. For a longer stay with a flexible itinerary, another area may offer a better overall fit. Compare the full stay cost rather than the advertised nightly rate alone.

Airport Connections and Hotel Location

For JFK arrivals, AirTrain connects the airport with subway and Long Island Rail Road connections. Choose your onward route based on the hotel address, luggage and station accessibility. Check current services before travel.

If arriving at LaGuardia or Newark, plan that airport's route separately. Do not assume all New York airports have the same rail connections. Allow time for transfers and the final walk to your hotel.

New York Hotel Planning FAQ

Where should I stay in New York for the first time?

Midtown is a useful starting point for central Manhattan sightseeing and Broadway. Compare it with other neighborhoods if your activities are mainly downtown, in Brooklyn or near Central Park.

Is staying near Times Square necessary?

No. Choose a location that fits your itinerary. Nearby Midtown neighborhoods or another area with suitable transport connections may work better for your preferences.

Is Long Island City always cheaper than Manhattan?

No. Prices depend on dates, room type and availability. Compare final totals and transport needs for the same trip.

Should I book a refundable hotel rate?

Consider one if your plans may change. Compare its additional cost with the cancellation deadline and refund terms.

How do I avoid booking in the wrong location?

Check the full address on a map and plan routes to your main activities. Confirm that the listing is in New York City and in your intended neighborhood.
