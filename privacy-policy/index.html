Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how GiftCardZoneEU uses website analytics, affiliate links and information you choose to send us. GiftCardZoneEU publishes independent travel guides and links to third-party booking services. We do not take bookings or payments on this website.

Affiliate disclosure: Some links are affiliate links. If you use one and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. Read our affiliate disclosure.

Information we process

When you visit the site, our hosting and analytics services may process technical information such as your IP address, device and browser details, approximate location, pages viewed and referral information. If you contact us, we process the information you include in your message so we can respond.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the website and improve its content and usability. These services may use cookies or similar technologies and may process usage and device information under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for website operation, analytics and referral tracking. Affiliate partners may use tracking when you follow a partner link. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where consent is required for optional cookies, those cookies should be used only after you have made a choice.

Affiliate and external links

When you click a partner link, you leave GiftCardZoneEU. The partner or affiliate network may receive information needed to record the referral. Booking, payment and customer support take place on the partner's website. Please read that website's privacy policy before providing personal information.

Why we use information

  • To operate and protect the website.
  • To understand site usage and improve our guides.
  • To track qualifying referrals through affiliate partners.
  • To respond when you contact us.

Data retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose. Analytics, hosting and affiliate providers may have their own retention settings and policies.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You may contact us using the details on our Contact page. You can also manage browser cookies and any cookie choices offered on this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use our Contact page.
