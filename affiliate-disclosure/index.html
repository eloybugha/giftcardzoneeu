Affiliate Disclosure

GiftCardZoneEU uses affiliate links in some travel guides, partner pages and recommendations. This page explains how those links work and how they help support the website.

Last updated: September 20, 2026
Clear disclosureIf you click an affiliate link on GiftCardZoneEU and complete an eligible booking or purchase, we may receive a commission. You do not pay extra because you used our link.

How affiliate links work

Some links on GiftCardZoneEU contain tracking information that allows a partner or affiliate network to recognize that a visitor came from our website. If you complete a qualifying transaction, the partner may pay GiftCardZoneEU a commission.

A click does not always result in a commission. Eligibility can depend on the partner's tracking period, your browser settings, the selected product or service, and the partner's affiliate terms.

Our affiliate partners

Affiliate links may lead to third-party travel and shopping services, including hotel-booking sites, flight-search services, transport platforms, VPN providers and selected online marketplaces. Examples may include Trip.com, Expedia, Agoda, CheapTickets, Cheapvuelos, 12Go and other partners featured on the website.

Our partner list may change over time as programs are added, removed or updated.

No additional cost to you

Using an affiliate link does not add a separate GiftCardZoneEU fee to your booking or purchase. However, prices, taxes, service charges, exchange rates and optional extras are set by the third-party provider and may change at any time.

Always review the final price, currency, cancellation policy, baggage conditions, refund rules and other important terms on the provider's website before paying.

Editorial independence

Affiliate commissions help support the operation and continued development of GiftCardZoneEU, but they do not guarantee a positive review, ranking or recommendation. We aim to provide useful, accurate and independent information so readers can compare options for themselves.

Placement on the website may be influenced by relevance, availability, reader usefulness and commercial relationships. We encourage visitors to compare more than one provider when appropriate.

Third-party responsibility

GiftCardZoneEU does not process reservations, issue tickets, take payments or control third-party products and services. The provider shown during checkout is responsible for the transaction, availability, customer service, changes, cancellations and refunds.

Names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. A reference to a company does not mean that GiftCardZoneEU owns, operates or represents that company.

Questions

If you have a question about this disclosure or notice an affiliate link that is not clearly identified, please visit our contact page. For information about data and tracking technologies, read our privacy policy.
