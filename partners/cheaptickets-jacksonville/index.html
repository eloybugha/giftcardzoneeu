Why visit Jacksonville?

Riverfront attractions, Atlantic beaches, sports and neighborhood escapes

Jacksonville combines a lively waterfront, historic districts, professional sports, museums, family attractions, cruise access and convenient connections to beaches and destinations across Florida’s Northeast Florida.

  • Walk along the St. Johns River and explore Downtown riverfront attractions.
  • Discover Riverside, Avondale and San Marco for dining, parks and local character.
  • Plan beach days at Jacksonville Beach, Neptune Beach or Atlantic Beach.
  • Attend football games, concerts, festivals and major events in the city.
  • Use Jacksonville as a base for Amelia Island, St. Augustine and Northeast Florida.

Where to stay

Popular Jacksonville areas for different trips

Choose a neighborhood based on your plans for sightseeing, dining, nightlife, sports, business travel or airport access.

Downtown Jacksonville

Convenient for the St. Johns River, museums, sports venues, business travel, events and central sightseeing.

Southbank

A riverfront area near hotels, the Riverwalk, museums, medical facilities and views across Downtown.

Riverside & Avondale

Known for historic homes, parks, cafés, restaurants, boutiques and a relaxed neighborhood atmosphere.

San Marco

A polished district with dining, shopping, river access and convenient connections to Downtown and Southbank.

Jacksonville Beach

Best for Atlantic Ocean access, beach activities, casual dining, nightlife and coastal stays.

JAX Airport Area

Practical for early departures, late arrivals, short stopovers and quick access to Jacksonville International Airport.
