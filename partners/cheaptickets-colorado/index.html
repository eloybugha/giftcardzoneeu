Why visit Colorado?

Creative cities, alpine scenery and mountain escapes

Colorado combines lively cities, Rocky Mountain landscapes, historic towns, alpine lakes and scenic road trips across one of the most distinctive destinations in the United States.

  • Explore Denver museums, dining, sports and vibrant neighborhoods.
  • Visit Colorado Springs for Garden of the Gods and Pikes Peak.
  • Choose Aspen or Vail for skiing, hiking and upscale mountain stays.
  • Experience Boulder trails, local dining and easy mountain access.
  • Combine a city stay with Rocky Mountain National Park or a scenic byway.

Where to stay

Popular Colorado areas for different trips

Choose a destination based on your plans for sightseeing, outdoor activities, dining, nightlife, business travel or airport access.

Downtown Denver

Ideal for museums, sports venues, restaurants, nightlife, conventions and convenient public transport connections.

Colorado Springs

Popular for Garden of the Gods, Pikes Peak, family attractions, military sites and access to scenic outdoor areas.

Aspen

A celebrated mountain destination for skiing, hiking, festivals, fine dining and scenic stays throughout the year.

Vail & Breckenridge

Well-known resort towns offering winter sports, summer trails, mountain villages and a wide range of accommodation.

Boulder

Great for hiking, university visits, local food, craft breweries and quick access to the foothills of the Rocky Mountains.

DEN Airport Area

Practical for early departures, late arrivals, short stopovers and road trips toward Denver, Boulder and the mountains.

Colorado travel planning

Useful details before booking

DEN and COS airports

DEN is Colorado's main international gateway, while COS is convenient for Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region. Both offer ground transport and car rentals.

Desert and outdoor attractions

Compare seasonal hotel demand around Denver, Aspen, Vail, Breckenridge, Rocky Mountain National Park and other mountain destinations.

Sports and events

Hotel demand can rise during major games, concerts, conventions, festivals and university events.

Getting around

Local transit varies by destination. Rideshare services are common in major cities, while a rental car can be useful for national parks and multi-city road trips.

Flexible dates

Adjusting travel dates, flight times or hotel areas may reveal different prices and booking conditions.

Booking protection

Save confirmation emails and review change or cancellation rules for every part of your reservation.

Frequently asked questions

CheapTickets Colorado booking FAQ

Can I compare Colorado flights and hotels with CheapTickets?

Yes. CheapTickets lets travelers search flights, hotels, vacation packages and car rentals for Colorado.

Which Colorado area is best for first-time visitors?

Denver suits travelers who want museums, dining and convenient transport. Choose Colorado Springs for family attractions, Boulder for foothill access, or Aspen, Vail and Breckenridge for mountain activities.

Which airport should I select for Colorado?

Denver International Airport uses the code DEN and is the state's main air gateway. Colorado Springs Airport uses COS and can be convenient for Colorado Springs, Pikes Peak and destinations in south-central Colorado.

Can a flight and hotel package be cheaper?

A package may provide competitive value for some dates. Compare the package total with separate flight and hotel prices.

When should I book a Colorado hotel?

Booking earlier may provide more choices during school holidays, weekends, major sporting events, festivals, conventions and holiday periods.

Who handles my reservation after booking?

CheapTickets or the provider shown in your confirmation handles payments, changes, cancellations and customer service.
