Why visit Tampa?

Waterfront attractions, historic neighborhoods, sports and family adventures

Tampa combines a lively waterfront, historic districts, professional sports, museums, family attractions, cruise access and convenient connections to beaches and destinations across Florida’s Gulf Coast.

  • Walk along the Tampa Riverwalk and explore Downtown waterfront attractions.
  • Discover the historic streets, restaurants and nightlife of Ybor City.
  • Plan family days at Busch Gardens Tampa Bay and nearby attractions.
  • Attend football, hockey, baseball, concerts or major events in the region.
  • Use Tampa as a base for Clearwater, St. Petersburg and Gulf Coast beaches.

Where to stay

Popular Tampa areas for different trips

Choose a neighborhood based on your plans for sightseeing, dining, nightlife, sports, business travel or airport access.

Downtown Tampa

Convenient for the Riverwalk, museums, event venues, waterfront attractions, business travel and central sightseeing.

Water Street

A modern waterfront district near dining, entertainment, Amalie Arena, the convention center and upscale hotels.

Ybor City

Known for historic architecture, Cuban heritage, restaurants, nightlife, live music and local character.

Hyde Park

A polished neighborhood with boutiques, cafés, restaurants and convenient access to Bayshore Boulevard.

Westshore

Useful for business travel, shopping, dining, airport access and stays between Downtown and TPA.

TPA Airport Area

Practical for early departures, late arrivals, short stopovers and travelers prioritizing quick airport access.
