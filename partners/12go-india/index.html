Important before booking

Confirm the exact station and boarding point

Large Indian cities may have several railway stations, terminals and pickup locations. Similar place names can refer to different departure points.

  • Check the full station or terminal name.
  • Confirm passenger names exactly as required.
  • Review baggage, identification and boarding rules.
  • Allow enough time to reach the departure point.

India travel planning

Useful checks before departure

Station names

Verify the exact railway station, bus terminal or pickup location shown in your booking confirmation.

Travel class

Check the seat, berth, air-conditioning and carriage details included with the selected ticket.

Identification

Carry the identification and travel documents required by the operator and shown in your booking instructions.

Boarding time

Arrive early enough to locate the platform, terminal or pickup point, especially at large transport hubs.

Ticket format

Review whether a mobile ticket is accepted or whether printed documents are required for the journey.

Booking protection

Save confirmation emails and review change, cancellation and refund conditions for each reservation.

Frequently asked questions

12Go India transport booking FAQ

Can I compare India train and bus tickets with 12Go?

Yes. 12Go may display available train, bus and transfer options for selected routes and dates. Availability depends on the operator and route.

Which India routes can I search?

Travelers can search selected connections involving cities such as Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Mumbai, Goa, Bengaluru and Chennai. Search results vary by date.

Does every booking include an electronic ticket?

Not necessarily. Ticket delivery and boarding requirements depend on the operator. Read the confirmation and carry any required printed documents.

How do I choose the correct railway station?

Review the complete station name and location shown in the search result. Major cities can have several stations, so do not rely only on the city name.

What should I check before paying?

Confirm passenger names, dates, stations, travel class, baggage conditions, cancellation rules and the complete price before payment.

Who handles my booking after payment?

12Go or the transport provider shown in your confirmation handles tickets, changes, cancellations, refunds and customer support.
