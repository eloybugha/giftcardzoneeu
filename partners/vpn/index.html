VPN basics

What does a VPN do?

A Virtual Private Network creates an encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server. This can make it harder for people on the same network to view your internet traffic and helps mask your regular IP address from the websites you visit.

  • Encrypts data travelling between your device and the VPN server
  • Adds protection when you connect to unsecured public Wi-Fi
  • Helps reduce exposure of your regular IP address while browsing

Before choosing

What should you compare?

Look beyond the headline price. A useful VPN should fit your devices, typical browsing habits and travel needs.

  • Supported devices and simultaneous connections
  • Server availability in the regions you need
  • Connection speed, privacy features and customer support

Quick answers

VPN frequently asked questions

Is a VPN useful on public Wi-Fi?

Yes. VPN encryption can add a layer of protection when you use shared networks in cafés, hotels, airports and other public places.

Does a VPN make me completely anonymous?

No. A VPN improves privacy, but it does not make you completely anonymous. Cookies, account logins, browser fingerprinting and unsafe downloads can still identify or harm you.

Can a VPN replace antivirus software?

No. A VPN protects your connection, while antivirus software is designed to detect and remove malicious files. They serve different purposes.
