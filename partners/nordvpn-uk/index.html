Helpful answers

NordVPN UK FAQ

Common questions about using NordVPN for privacy and internet security.

What does NordVPN do?

NordVPN encrypts internet traffic and routes it through a VPN server. This changes the visible IP address and adds privacy and security to the connection.

Can I use NordVPN on public Wi-Fi in the UK?

Yes. A VPN is useful when connecting to shared Wi-Fi networks in cafés, hotels, airports, train stations and coworking spaces.

How many devices can use one NordVPN account?

NordVPN currently supports up to ten simultaneous device connections. Limits and included features may change, so confirm the latest information before purchasing.

Does NordVPN make me completely anonymous?

No VPN provides complete anonymity. Browser cookies, account logins, device settings and online activity may still be used to identify a user.

Will NordVPN always increase internet speed?

Not necessarily. VPN performance depends on factors including your normal connection, chosen server, distance, network congestion and device.

Is NordVPN free?

NordVPN is primarily a paid VPN service. Plans, prices, discounts and included features can vary over time and by location.
