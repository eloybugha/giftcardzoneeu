Why visit Hamburg?

Historic canals, waterfront landmarks and lively districts

Hamburg blends maritime history with striking modern architecture, canals, museums, shopping, live music and neighborhoods with distinct personalities. Its compact centre makes it well suited to short city breaks and longer stays.

  • Explore Speicherstadt warehouses, canals and nearby HafenCity.
  • See the Elbphilharmonie and walk along the Elbe waterfront.
  • Visit Altstadt sights around Rathausmarkt and Mönckebergstraße.
  • Choose St. Pauli for nightlife, music and harbour atmosphere.
  • Relax beside the Inner and Outer Alster lakes.

Where to stay

Where to stay in Hamburg

Choose a destination based on your plans for sightseeing, outdoor activities, dining, nightlife, business travel or airport access.

Altstadt & Neustadt

Strong choices for first-time visitors who want central sightseeing, shopping, Rathausmarkt and convenient transport links.

HafenCity & Speicherstadt

Ideal for modern architecture, historic warehouses, canals, the Elbphilharmonie and waterfront walks.

St. Pauli

Best suited to travelers seeking nightlife, live music, Reeperbahn entertainment and harbour access.

St. Georg

Convenient for Hamburg Central Station, city transport, restaurants and access to the Außenalster.

Sternschanze

A lively neighborhood with independent cafés, bars, casual dining and an alternative local atmosphere.

Airport & North Hamburg

Practical for early flights, short stopovers, business travel and access to Hamburg Airport.

Hamburg travel planning

Useful details before booking

HAM Airport access

Hamburg Airport (HAM) connects with Hamburg Central Station via the S1 S-Bahn. The normal journey is about 25 minutes, with taxi and rental-car options also available.

City-break seasons

Prices and availability may change around summer weekends, trade fairs, school holidays, major events and the festive market season.

Sports and events

Hotel demand can rise during major games, concerts, conventions, festivals and university events.

Getting around

Local transit varies by destination. Rideshare services are common in major cities, while a rental car can be useful for national parks and multi-city road trips.

Which Hamburg area is best for first-time visitors?

Altstadt or Neustadt works well for central sightseeing. HafenCity and Speicherstadt suit waterfront visits, while St. Pauli is better for nightlife and St. Georg is convenient for the central railway station.

Which airport should I select for Hamburg?

Hamburg Airport uses the code HAM and is the city's main airport. The S1 S-Bahn normally reaches Hamburg Central Station in about 25 minutes.
