Cologne Hotel Guide

Why stay in Cologne?

Cologne, locally known as Köln, is one of Germany’s most recognizable city destinations. Its skyline is dominated by the twin towers of Cologne Cathedral, while the Rhine River, historic streets and lively neighborhoods give visitors plenty to explore.

Staying near the city center places many leading attractions within walking distance. Travelers attending an exhibition or concert may prefer Deutz, while visitors looking for independent cafés, nightlife and a more local atmosphere can consider Ehrenfeld.

Cologne at a glance

  • Home to the UNESCO-listed Cologne Cathedral
  • Excellent rail links to other German and European cities
  • Popular for museums, events and Christmas markets
  • Walkable central districts along the Rhine River
  • Accommodation for leisure, family and business trips

Frequently Asked Questions

Cologne hotel FAQs

Helpful answers for planning where and when to stay in Cologne.

What is the best area to stay in Cologne for first-time visitors?

Cologne Old Town is a convenient choice because it places you near Cologne Cathedral, the Rhine promenade, museums, restaurants and Cologne Central Station.

Is Deutz a good place to stay in Cologne?

Yes. Deutz is particularly practical for Koelnmesse, Lanxess Arena and events. The historic center is just across the Rhine and can be reached by train, tram or on foot.

How many days should I spend in Cologne?

Two or three days are usually enough for Cologne Cathedral, the Old Town, the Rhine waterfront and several museums or neighborhood visits.

When should I book my Cologne hotel?

Booking early is recommended for summer weekends, trade fairs, concerts, Carnival and the Christmas market season, when demand may increase.
