Where to board in Baltimore

Amtrak from Baltimore Penn Station

Choose Baltimore Penn Station (BAL), 1500 North Charles Street, for the city departure. BWI Rail Station is a separate stop. If you see both in a fare search, check the station code before paying. Amtrak recommends reaching Penn at least 30 minutes before departure, or 45 minutes if you need ticketing or baggage assistance.

FlixBus from Haines Street or White Marsh

A ticket marked Baltimore Downtown Bus Station takes you to 2110 Haines Street. For White Marsh Park N Ride, use 8476 Honeygo Boulevard. If you’re leaving from an Inner Harbor hotel, compare the ride to each pickup point before choosing the lower fare.

For Peter Pan, open the individual departure and use the pickup address on your booking. The city name alone is too broad to use as a rideshare destination.

Where you arrive in Philadelphia

30th Street Station for University City or another Amtrak train

Amtrak arrives at William H. Gray III 30th Street Station (PHL), 2955 Market Street. This is the more convenient arrival if you’re meeting someone at the rail station or continuing by Amtrak. For a separately booked train to New York, allow time for a late arrival and check the departure board for your next gate.

Filbert Street for a Center City stay

FlixBus lists Philadelphia Bus Station, 1001 Filbert Street. If you’re staying near Reading Terminal Market or the convention center, map the walk from this address. If someone is picking you up, send them the street address printed on your bus ticket.

Arriving by bus and continuing by Amtrak? A Filbert Street arrival and a 30th Street rail departure require a transfer across town. Include that transfer when comparing the bus with a through rail itinerary.

How to compare tickets for your date

For an early or late train, search Baltimore Penn (BAL) to Philadelphia (PHL) on Amtrak. The Night Owl offer covers select Coach departures between 6 PM and 8 AM. Open your preferred train to see whether the discounted fare is available.

For a bus, enter your date and passenger count, then open the departure details. Compare the pickup address, Philadelphia drop-off, baggage allowance and final total. If you have a suitcase and a hotel near Filbert Street, include the cost of reaching the hotel from each arrival point.

Baltimore to Philadelphia FAQ

How long is the train from Baltimore to Philadelphia?

One November 2026 timetable example leaves Baltimore Penn at 6:08 AM and arrives in Philadelphia at 7:16 AM, a ride of 1 hour 8 minutes. Check Amtrak for the running time on your travel date.

Can the train cost less than the bus?

Yes. Amtrak lists a $10 one-way Night Owl sample fare between Baltimore and Philadelphia. It can beat the advertised FlixBus starting fare of $18.48 when a discounted Coach seat is available and the departure time suits you.

Which Philadelphia stop should I choose for a Center City hotel?

For a hotel near the convention center, start by checking the walk from 1001 Filbert Street. For an address near the University of Pennsylvania, compare the trip from 30th Street Station. Enter your hotel’s street address to see which arrival saves you a transfer.

Can I continue from Philadelphia to New York?

Yes. If both legs are by Amtrak, look for an itinerary through Philadelphia when booking. If you arrive by coach, check the departure address on your New York ticket before leaving the first bus terminal.
