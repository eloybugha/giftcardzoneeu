New York to Baltimore by Train or Bus: Penn Station, Downtown and White Marsh

Staying at the Inner Harbor? Start with two arrival points: Baltimore Penn Station for Amtrak and Haines Street for a downtown bus. In our Thanksgiving morning sample, a Midtown bus reached Haines Street just 17 minutes after the train reached Penn Station. The hotel transfer could decide which passenger reached the waterfront first.

Where to board in Manhattan

Amtrak from New York Penn Station (NYP)

Select NYP as the origin and BAL as the destination. Moynihan Train Hall is across Eighth Avenue from the original Penn Station complex. For an early train, check Amtrak’s station hours and entrance information before arranging your ride.

FlixBus from West 31st Street and Eighth Avenue

The two sample buses use the Midtown street stop. Follow the boarding instructions on your ticket to the correct side of the street. A reservation showing Port Authority or a different Manhattan stop has its own boarding point.

Which Baltimore arrival stop should you choose?

Baltimore Penn Station · 1500 North Charles Street

Choose BAL if you want to arrive by train and continue toward Mount Vernon or the waterfront. Meeting a driver? Amtrak identifies the pickup area on the east side of the station near the main entrance.

Baltimore Downtown Bus Station · 2110 Haines Street

This is the arrival point for both buses in the table. Use the street address when estimating the ride to your hotel. The word “Downtown” on the ticket can make it easy to overlook that last leg.

White Marsh Park & Ride · 8476 Honeygo Boulevard

Greyhound also lists White Marsh on this route. It makes sense if someone is collecting you there or you are staying nearby. For a waterfront stay, price the onward ride from Honeygo Boulevard before choosing a White Marsh departure.

Which ticket works for an Inner Harbor hotel?

From Penn Station, the city’s Charm City Circulator map shows the Purple Route serving both the station and the Inner Harbor. Check southbound service for your arrival day, then add the walk from the stop to your hotel entrance.

From Haines Street, use 2110 Haines Street as the starting point for a transit or rideshare estimate at your expected arrival time. For the 8:40 AM bus in the table, the ticket saved $200 compared with the early train. The train’s 17-minute lead applied only as far as the two arrival stations.

If someone can pick you up at White Marsh, send them the stop address first. For a group sharing a ride, compare the total ticket cost for everyone plus one vehicle fare.

New York to Baltimore FAQ

How long is the train from New York to Baltimore?

The Northeast Regional in our sample took 2 hours 48 minutes from Penn Station in New York to Penn Station in Baltimore. Check the running time of the specific train you select.

Does the New York bus drop me at the Inner Harbor?

The two example tickets end at 2110 Haines Street. Plan a separate connection to your waterfront destination. Check the arrival address again if you change departures.

Is White Marsh the same stop as Baltimore Downtown?

They are separate stops. White Marsh is on Honeygo Boulevard. Baltimore Downtown Bus Station is on Haines Street. Use the ticketed stop when arranging pickup.

Search buses on 12Go and check NYP to BAL on Amtrak. Compare the final fare, baggage terms and arrival address for the departure you intend to take.
