Where to board in Boston

Amtrak from South Station or Back Bay

For a downtown departure, search Boston South Station (BOS). If you are staying near Copley Square, search Boston Back Bay (BBY) and map the walk to 145 Dartmouth Street. Select New York Penn Station (NYP) as your destination.

If both Boston stations appear on the same train’s itinerary, use the departure time beside the station printed on your ticket. This matters when arranging an early hotel checkout or a ride to the station.

Bus from South Station

If your FlixBus ticket says Boston (South Station), use the bus terminal address, 700 Atlantic Avenue. Give that address to your driver and allow time to find the gate inside. For Peter Pan, follow the terminal and boarding details on your reservation.

Which Manhattan arrival is closest to your hotel?

  • Penn Station or Madison Square Garden. Amtrak serves New York Penn Station (NYP). For a bus, look for the New York Midtown stop at 300 West 31st Street, across from Moynihan Train Hall.
  • Times Square or the Theater District. If the bus serves Port Authority, map your walk from 625 Eighth Avenue. Use your hotel’s street entrance as the destination, especially if you are carrying luggage.
  • Hudson Yards. FlixBus also lists a stop at 600 West 34th Street. Check that this address appears on the departure you choose.

Meeting someone at Penn Station? Send them the operator and the exact meeting point. A traveler arriving at the West 31st Street bus stop will be outdoors, while a train passenger can arrange to meet inside Moynihan Train Hall.

Boston to New York FAQ

How long is the train from Boston to New York?

The two Amtrak examples took 3 hours 46 minutes and 4 hours 21 minutes. Check the scheduled journey from your chosen Boston station to New York Penn Station.

Which Boston station should I use if I am staying near Copley Square?

For Amtrak, look for Boston Back Bay (BBY) at 145 Dartmouth Street. Select Back Bay when booking so your ticket shows the departure time for that station.

Can I take a bus from Boston to a stop near Penn Station?

Look for a departure serving New York Midtown at 300 West 31st Street. This stop is across from Moynihan Train Hall. Other departures can use different stops, including Port Authority.
