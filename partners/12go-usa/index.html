Frequently asked questions

Questions about booking USA transport

Helpful information before searching for trains, buses, ferries and transfers.

Can I book USA train and bus tickets through 12Go?

Travelers can use 12Go to search and compare supported train, bus, ferry and transfer options in the United States. Availability depends on the route, date and transport operator.

Which USA routes can I search?

Supported searches may include routes involving New York, Washington DC, Boston, Philadelphia, Chicago, Milwaukee, Los Angeles, San Diego, Miami, Orlando, San Francisco and other destinations.

Does GiftCardZoneEU process my payment?

No. GiftCardZoneEU provides information and referral links. Search results, payment, booking confirmation, cancellation and customer service are handled by 12Go and the selected transport provider.

Are transport prices and schedules guaranteed?

No. Prices, schedules, availability, baggage policies and booking terms can change. Always confirm the final information on 12Go before paying.

Can I search for airport transfers?

Airport transfer options may appear for supported cities and travel dates. Available vehicles, pickup locations and transfer conditions depend on the provider.

Will I receive a booking confirmation?

Confirmation details are provided according to the selected journey and operator. Review the confirmation instructions sent by 12Go after completing your booking.

Can I cancel or change my ticket?

Change, cancellation and refund rules vary by ticket and operator. Read the fare conditions carefully before completing payment and contact 12Go support for booking-related assistance.

Does GiftCardZoneEU earn a commission?

GiftCardZoneEU may earn a commission from eligible bookings made through affiliate links, at no additional cost to the traveler.
