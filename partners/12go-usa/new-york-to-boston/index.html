South Station or Back Bay for your hotel?

Back Bay for Copley Square

Enter Boston Back Bay (BBY) as your Amtrak destination. Map the walk from 145 Dartmouth Street to your hotel. This station is handy for Copley Square and the Boston Public Library.

South Station for downtown

Search for Boston South Station (BOS) if you want a train into the Financial District. For a hotel across Fort Point Channel, map the walk to its entrance. A Seaport street address can still be a substantial walk with luggage.

Where to board the bus in New York

FlixBus lists several boarding points for this route. Two Manhattan stops have different entrances even though both are near Eighth Avenue.

  • New York Midtown: 300 West 31st Street, across from Moynihan Train Hall. If you have just arrived by train, use the street address to find the outdoor boarding area.
  • Port Authority Bus Terminal: 625 Eighth Avenue. Allow time to get inside and locate the departure gate shown for your bus.

In Boston, FlixBus lists its South Station bus terminal at 700 Atlantic Avenue. It also lists a Brookline stop, so check the destination on your ticket before arranging pickup. For a Peter Pan booking, use the stops on that operator’s itinerary.

New York City to Boston FAQ

How long is the train from New York City to Boston?

The 5:27 AM and 5:44 AM trains in our comparison took 4 hours 5 minutes and 4 hours 21 minutes. The 9:00 AM example took 4 hours 38 minutes. Check the arrival time for the Boston station you select.

Which Boston station should I choose for Copley Square?

Look for Boston Back Bay (BBY). Its Dartmouth Street entrance is near Copley Square and the Boston Public Library.

Where do I meet someone arriving by bus at Boston South Station?

If the ticket lists the South Station bus terminal, use 700 Atlantic Avenue when arranging pickup. Agree on a meeting point in the bus terminal.
