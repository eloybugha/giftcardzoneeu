Taking Amtrak from Manhattan

Enter New York, NY (NYP) and Philadelphia, PA (PHL) in the train search. In Manhattan, look for New York Penn Station and Moynihan Train Hall. In Philadelphia, your arrival is William H. Gray III 30th Street Station. On an Amtrak ticket, PHL refers to this rail station.

For a pre-dawn departure, check Amtrak’s New York station page for entrance hours. Keep the train number handy so you can match your booking to the departure board.

Taking the bus and finding your stop

One New York boarding option is Port Authority Bus Terminal, 625 Eighth Avenue. Use that address only if your ticket names Port Authority. Other departures use different stops around New York.

In Philadelphia, FlixBus lists 1001 Filbert Street for Philadelphia Bus Station and 2955 Market Street for its 30th Street stop. Check the stop on each leg separately, especially if you take the train one way and the bus back.

New York to Philadelphia FAQ

Is there a direct train from New York to Philadelphia?

Yes. Search NYP to PHL for a direct Amtrak train from New York Penn Station to Philadelphia’s 30th Street Station.

Is the bus always cheaper than Amtrak?

Compare both for the same date. Discounted train tickets can cost less than the bus, particularly if you can travel early. Compare the final price and change terms before paying.

Do all buses arrive at 30th Street Station?

No. FlixBus lists several Philadelphia stops. A ticket for 1001 Filbert Street takes you to a different part of the city than a ticket for 2955 Market Street.

Can I do Philadelphia as a day trip from New York?

Yes. Choose the return departure before booking the outbound trip. For a short visit, compare how much time each option leaves between arrival and the journey back to your departure stop.
