Baltimore to Washington, DC by Train or Bus

The MARC train costs $9 one way from either Baltimore Penn Station or Camden Yards to Washington Union Station. If you’re staying near the Inner Harbor on a weekday, check Camden first. On Saturday or Sunday, head to Penn Station for MARC weekend service.

Camden Yards, Penn Station or Haines Street?

Camden Station for a weekday departure near the ballpark

If you’re staying between the Inner Harbor and Oriole Park, check the walk from your hotel to Camden Station. The MARC Camden Line goes directly to Washington Union Station on weekdays. Check the next departure before setting out. A shorter walk offers little advantage if the next train leaves several hours later.

Baltimore Penn Station for weekend MARC or Amtrak

Use 1500 North Charles Street, Baltimore for a ride to Penn Station. Both MARC Penn Line and Amtrak use this station. If you arrived here on Amtrak from New York, you can start the next leg to DC from the same building. In the MARC timetable, choose Penn Station. West Baltimore and BWI Rail Station are separate stops.

Haines Street for a Downtown Bus Station ticket

If your bus ticket says Baltimore Downtown Bus Station, use 2110 Haines Street. Compare the ride from your hotel with the cost of reaching Penn or Camden. For any other boarding point, use the address printed on that departure’s ticket.

Where to buy Baltimore to DC tickets

MARC. Buy through Maryland MTA’s CharmPass app or use a MARC ticket machine at Penn Station or Camden Yards. The published adult one-way fare to Washington is $9 from either Baltimore station. MTA lists a $5 onboard surcharge when boarding at a station with a ticket machine or agent.

Amtrak. Search Baltimore Penn (BAL) → Washington Union Station (WAS) for your date. If a MARC departure misses your connection, compare the Amtrak arrival time and fare before changing your plans.

Bus. Open the individual departure on 12Go to check the operator, pickup point, DC drop-off and baggage allowance. Add the cost of reaching the stop when comparing it with the $9 train.

Arriving at Washington Union Station

MARC and Amtrak arrive at Washington Union Station, 50 Massachusetts Avenue NE. If someone is meeting you, agree on an entrance before the trip. A bus ticket marked DC (Union Station) uses the station’s bus facilities, so a rail-platform meeting point can send your driver to the wrong place.

For a same-day return, open the northbound timetable before leaving Baltimore. The examples above all run toward Washington. Check your return departure from Union Station and which Baltimore station it serves.

Baltimore to Washington, DC FAQ

How much is the train from Baltimore to Washington, DC?

Maryland MTA lists the standard adult MARC fare at $9 one way from Baltimore Penn or Camden Station to Washington. Amtrak sets a separate fare for the train and date you choose.

Does the Camden Line run from Baltimore to DC on Sunday?

The Camden Line runs on weekdays. For Sunday MARC service, travel from Baltimore Penn Station. The published timetable shows train 677 leaving at 8:55 AM and reaching Washington at 9:55 AM.

How long is the MARC train ride?

The weekend Penn Line departures shown here take 60 or 61 minutes. Weekday Camden Line train 843 takes 59 minutes. Allow additional time at both ends for the trip between your accommodation and the station.

Which station should I use from an Inner Harbor hotel?

On a weekday, compare your walk to Camden Station with the next Camden Line departure. On weekends, use Baltimore Penn for MARC. If you choose a bus, enter the boarding address from your ticket when planning the hotel transfer.
