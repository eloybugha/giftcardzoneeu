Taking Amtrak from Philadelphia’s 30th Street Station

Search for Philadelphia (PHL) → Washington Union Station (WAS). Your Philadelphia departure station is William H. Gray III 30th Street Station. If you arrive there by train from New York, use the train number on your Washington booking to find your next departure on the board.

In DC, Union Station is at 50 Massachusetts Avenue NE. Use that address for a pickup or to plan your walk toward the Capitol. For the return journey, Amtrak says the boarding gate closes three minutes before departure. Allow time to get inside and find your gate.

Finding your bus in Philadelphia and DC

If your ticket says Philadelphia Bus Station, FlixBus lists the address as 1001 Filbert Street. If you’re arriving at 30th Street Station by train, allow time to travel between the two terminals before your bus leaves.

For a ticket marked Washington DC (Dupont Circle), the listed stop is 1600 Connecticut Avenue NW. Open the stop details for each direction when booking a round trip. The boarding point on the way back depends on the departure you choose.

Philadelphia to Washington, DC FAQ

How long is the train from Philadelphia to Washington, DC?
