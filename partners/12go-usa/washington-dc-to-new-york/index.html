Amtrak from Washington Union Station to New York Penn

Search for Washington Union Station (WAS) → New York Penn Station (NYP). The DC station is at 50 Massachusetts Avenue NE. If your Philadelphia train brought you into Union Station, look up the New York train number on the departure board.

In Manhattan, Moynihan Train Hall is on Eighth Avenue between West 31st and West 33rd Streets. If someone is meeting you, agree on a street corner or a hall entrance before you arrive. “Penn Station” alone leaves several places to look.

Which bus stop is closest to your hotel?

Check both ends of the ticket. In DC, FlixBus lists Union Station at 50 Massachusetts Avenue NE and Dupont Circle at 1600 Connecticut Avenue NW. At Union Station, follow the bus signs for your departure.

  • Penn Station or Madison Square Garden: Look for New York Midtown (31st St & 8th Ave), at 300 West 31st Street, across from Moynihan Train Hall.
  • Times Square: Compare a trip serving Port Authority at 625 Eighth Avenue. Use the terminal address when mapping the walk to your hotel.
  • Hudson Yards: FlixBus lists a stop at 600 West 34th Street. Match the street number to your ticket because other nearby stops have similar names.

Washington, DC to New York FAQ

How long is the train from DC to New York?

The two morning examples took 2 hours 54 minutes and 3 hours 34 minutes. Check the scheduled time for your train.

Which New York station should I book for Manhattan?

Choose New York Penn Station (NYP) for Amtrak. Moynihan Train Hall serves this station.

Does the bus arrive at Penn Station?

Some buses use the Midtown stop at 300 West 31st Street, across from Moynihan Train Hall. Other trips use different stops. Check the address on your ticket.
