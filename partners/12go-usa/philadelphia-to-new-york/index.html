Is it worth catching the early train?

For an early appointment

The 5:52 AM Amtrak train was scheduled to reach New York at 7:26 AM for $15.91. For an 8 AM appointment, that leaves 34 minutes to get off the train and reach your destination. Check the walk or subway connection from Penn Station before booking.

For a later start

The 9 AM Coach Express bus was scheduled to arrive at 11 AM for $19.50. The Peter Pan bus left 70 minutes later and cost just 25 cents more. Compare the pickup addresses first. The later bus may also be easier to reach from your hotel.

Where to board in Philadelphia

Amtrak at 30th Street Station

Choose William H. Gray III 30th Street Station (PHL) as your departure station and New York Penn Station (NYP) as your destination. If you are arranging a ride before dawn, give the driver the full station name and check Amtrak’s station page for the current drop-off point.

Peter Pan in Center City

Peter Pan lists the Philadelphia Bus Terminal at 1001 Filbert Street, Philadelphia, PA 19107. The operator lists Gate 3 for departures. Check the display inside the terminal because the gate can change.

Coach Express pickup

The city-to-city timetable shows a 9 AM departure without a street address. Open the selected trip and confirm both addresses before paying. Use the Filbert Street directions above only if that address appears on your ticket.

Which Manhattan stop fits your plans?

Penn Station or Madison Square Garden. Amtrak serves New York Penn Station (NYP). If someone is meeting you at Moynihan Train Hall, agree on a specific entrance or landmark and send them your train number.

Times Square or the Theater District. For a Peter Pan ticket showing Port Authority, map your onward walk from 625 Eighth Avenue, between West 40th and West 42nd streets. Use your hotel’s street entrance as the destination.

Heading downtown? Compare the onward journey from each ticket’s named stop, especially if you have luggage. A bus advertised simply as arriving in “New York” may leave you with a different subway ride from someone arriving by train.
