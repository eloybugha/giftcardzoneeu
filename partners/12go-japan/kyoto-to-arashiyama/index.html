Kyoto to Arashiyama: JR, Hankyu or Randen for the Bamboo Grove?

Choose your train around where you are staying in Kyoto. Arrive by JR, Hankyu or Randen, then connect the Bamboo Grove, Tenryu-ji and Togetsukyo Bridge into a relaxed walking route.

Quick answer: start with the railway near your hotel

Near Kyoto Station? Take JR to Saga-Arashiyama. Near Kyoto-Kawaramachi or Karasuma? Compare Hankyu via Katsura. Near Shijo-Omiya? Take Randen directly to Arashiyama.

Kyoto Station: JR

A direct conventional train journey. Follow Sagano Line signs, then walk from Saga-Arashiyama toward the sightseeing area.

Shijo area: compare your nearest stop

Hankyu is useful from Kawaramachi or Karasuma. Randen is convenient from Shijo-Omiya and arrives close to Tenryu-ji.

The station name matters. JR Saga-Arashiyama, Hankyu Arashiyama and Randen Arashiyama are three different places. Include the operator when searching directions.

Where to board and change

JR: Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama

Use the conventional JR gates and follow signs for the Sagano Line, part of the San-in Main Line. Check that your service stops at Saga-Arashiyama. Allow time to reach the platform; Kyoto Station is large.

Hankyu: change at Katsura

From Kyoto-Kawaramachi or Karasuma, take an Osaka-bound Kyoto Line service stopping at Katsura. Change there for the Arashiyama Line and continue to its terminus. Check the departure board rather than relying on a fixed platform number.

Randen: board at Shijo-Omiya

Use the Randen station at Shijo-Omiya for a direct Arashiyama Line ride. Hankyu Omiya is a separate station nearby. If starting on Randen’s Kitano Line instead, check the connection at Katabiranotsuji.

Do not confuse the two Sagano trains. The regular JR Sagano Line brings you from Kyoto Station. The Sagano Romantic Train is a separate sightseeing service; plan its tickets and operating dates separately.

Suggested half-day: bamboo, garden and river

Allow roughly four to five hours in the area, plus your journeys from and back to your accommodation. This is a planning suggestion, not a fixed tour timetable.

  1. Arrive and check your return station. Save the correct operator on your map. From Hankyu, you can make the bridge your first stop on the way into the area.
  2. Walk through the Bamboo Grove. Go at a comfortable pace and keep the path clear for others. An early start may help with crowds, but does not guarantee quiet paths.
  3. Add Tenryu-ji if you want a garden visit. Use the admission entrance and allow time to enjoy the grounds rather than treating them only as a shortcut.
  4. Take a lunch or tea break. Choose a stop around its menu and queue. Leave flexibility instead of scheduling every minute.
  5. Finish by Togetsukyo Bridge and the river. Adjust the order around your arrival and return stations. Allow more time if you add another temple or a separate sightseeing activity.

Returning to Kyoto

For Kyoto Station, use JR Saga-Arashiyama and a Kyoto-bound Sagano Line train. For Kyoto-Kawaramachi or Karasuma, take Hankyu to Katsura and change to a suitable Kyoto-bound service. For Shijo-Omiya, use Randen’s Arashiyama Line.

You can return by a different operator if you pay the appropriate fare or hold valid coverage. Check the final stop and transfers before leaving, especially if you stay for dinner.

Kyoto to Arashiyama FAQ

Which train should I take from Kyoto to Arashiyama?

From Kyoto Station, compare direct JR Sagano Line trains to Saga-Arashiyama. From Kyoto-Kawaramachi or Karasuma, use Hankyu via Katsura. From Shijo-Omiya, Randen offers a direct ride to its central Arashiyama station.

Are the three Arashiyama stations the same?

No. JR Saga-Arashiyama, Randen Arashiyama and Hankyu Arashiyama are separate stations. Hankyu is south of the river; Randen is near Tenryu-ji. Save the operator as well as the station name.

Where do I change on the Hankyu route?

Change at Katsura from the Kyoto Line to the Arashiyama Line. Follow the departure displays for Arashiyama.

Is the Sagano Romantic Train the train from Kyoto Station?

No. The JR Sagano Line is the regular access route from Kyoto Station. The Sagano Romantic Train is a separate sightseeing service with separate tickets and operating arrangements.

Can I visit Arashiyama in half a day?

Allow roughly four to five hours in the area for the Bamboo Grove, Tenryu-ji and Togetsukyo Bridge with a short food stop. This is a suggested planning allowance; add time for crowds, a slower pace or extra attractions.

Do ordinary train seats need reservations?

Ordinary seating on these local access routes does not need a reservation, and seats are not guaranteed. Premium seating or separate sightseeing trains have their own ticket rules.
