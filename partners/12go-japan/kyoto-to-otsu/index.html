Kyoto to Otsu: JR or Keihan for Lake Biwa?

Choose JR from Kyoto Station or a subway and Keihan through train from Sanjo Keihan. Compare fares, arrival stations and the final walk to Otsu Port before planning your lakeside outing.

Quick answer: Kyoto Station or downtown Kyoto?

Near Kyoto Station? Take JR to Otsu, then walk towards the lake. Near Sanjo? Compare the Tozai Subway and Keihan through service to Biwako-hamaotsu, which puts you closer to Otsu Port.

From Kyoto Station

JR takes about 9–10 minutes to Otsu. The adult one-way reference fare is ¥200. Add your walk from the station to the waterfront.

From Sanjo Keihan

A direct Biwako-hamaotsu-bound train takes about 23–25 minutes. The adult one-way reference fare is ¥470 across the subway and Keihan sections.

Choose the lake destination first. This guide covers central Otsu and Otsu Port. Lake Biwa is much larger than this area; a hotel or attraction elsewhere may need a different station.

JR: Kyoto Station to Otsu

At Kyoto Station, follow the JR Biwako Line departure information and choose a train whose stop list includes Otsu (大津). Use the platform shown for that departure rather than relying on a fixed platform number.

This is a short conventional-rail journey. A Shinkansen ticket is not needed. Check the destination and stops before boarding: searching only for “Lake Biwa” does not identify a station.

JR is a practical choice for a low-cost outing from the Kyoto Station area. If you plan to return to the same area, save the Otsu → Kyoto journey before setting off on your lakeside walk.

Downtown route: Sanjo Keihan to Biwako-hamaotsu

Use Sanjo Keihan subway station for the Tozai Line. If arriving at Keihan's nearby Sanjo station, allow time to walk to the subway gates; the names refer to different stations.

Look for a train displaying Biwako-hamaotsu (びわ湖浜大津). A through service continues from the Tozai Line onto Keihan's Keishin Line at Misasagi without requiring you to leave the train.

Not every eastbound subway train goes to Otsu. If the train is bound for Rokujizo, a journey planner may direct you to change at Misasagi. Check the actual route before boarding.

One train, two operators. The ¥470 reference covers this complete Sanjo Keihan–Biwako-hamaotsu journey. A subway-only ticket or pass should not be assumed to cover the Keihan section.

Getting to Otsu Port and the waterfront

The Otsu tourism association gives about 15 minutes on foot from JR Otsu to Otsu Port . Allow around 15–20 minutes at a relaxed pace, and more if you stop along the way.

From Biwako-hamaotsu, the port is roughly 3–5 minutes away on foot. Follow local signs and the walking route to your actual pier or meeting point.

For a simple outing, use Otsu Port as your first map pin, then choose a manageable waterfront walk and a lunch or café stop. Decide how far to walk before you need to turn back for your train.

The train arrival time is not your cruise check-in time. Add the station walk and the operator's required boarding margin when booking a boat trip.

Optional Lake Biwa cruise from Otsu Port

Biwako Kisen operates Michigan cruise courses from Otsu Port, including 60- and 90-minute options on the published schedule. Available courses and departure times depend on the date; winter arrangements can differ.

Check the operator's current timetable and fares before planning around a sailing. Confirm the boarding port, return port, course duration and whether your ticket includes food.

Cruise tickets are separate from rail tickets. Weather, private charters or operational changes can affect sailings, so keep a lakeside walk or café stop as an alternative.

A flexible half-day Otsu plan

  1. Choose your departure station. Start with JR near Kyoto Station or the through route near Sanjo Keihan.
  2. Walk to Otsu Port. Use the appropriate station-to-port walking allowance and leave time for orientation.
  3. Spend time beside the lake. Our suggestion is 45–60 minutes for a relaxed walk and photographs, adjusted for weather and your pace.
  4. Add lunch or one booked activity. A cruise needs its own boarding buffer; check the sailing before fixing your train departure.
  5. Return to the Kyoto area you need. Choose JR Otsu for Kyoto Station or Biwako-hamaotsu for the downtown subway connection.

This is a suggested sequence, not a timed tour. Allow around three to four hours for an easy walk-and-lunch outing from your Kyoto departure station, and more if adding a cruise or other attraction.

Practical tips for this route

  • Save the Japanese station names. 大津 and びわ湖浜大津 identify the two different arrivals used here.
  • Check the train's destination at Sanjo Keihan. A Biwako-hamaotsu through service makes the downtown route simpler.
  • Pack for an exposed waterfront. Choose clothing for the day's wind and rain, particularly if spending time on a boat deck.
  • Check step-free routes if needed. Allow extra station time when travelling with a stroller, wheelchair or heavy luggage.
  • Leave room for the return walk. A short rail journey does not remove the time needed to reach the station.

Kyoto to Otsu FAQ

What is the easiest way from Kyoto Station to Otsu?

Take a JR Biwako Line train that stops at Otsu. The train ride is about 9–10 minutes, with a ¥200 adult one-way reference fare. Add the walk to your lakeside destination.

Can I travel directly from downtown Kyoto to Biwako-hamaotsu?

Yes. From Sanjo Keihan, choose a Tozai Subway through service bound for Biwako-hamaotsu. It continues onto the Keihan Keishin Line without a train change. The reference journey is about 23–25 minutes and ¥470 one way.

Are JR Otsu and Biwako-hamaotsu the same station?

No. JR Otsu is the arrival used from Kyoto Station. Biwako-hamaotsu is a separate Keihan station closer to Otsu Port. Choose based on your starting point and final destination.

Do I need a Shinkansen ticket for Kyoto to Otsu?

No. The JR route in this guide uses ordinary conventional trains. The alternative uses the Kyoto subway and Keihan railway.

Can I use JR one way and Keihan the other?

Yes, with the appropriate tickets. The reference rail total is ¥670, but the two Kyoto endpoints differ: Kyoto Station for JR and Sanjo Keihan for the subway route. Add any further connection you need.
