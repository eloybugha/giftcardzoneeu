UK booking FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can I compare train, coach and ferry tickets for the UK through 12Go?
12Go may display available trains, coaches, ferries and private transfers for selected UK routes. Availability varies by destination, operator and travel date.
Which UK destinations can I search through 12Go?
Popular searches may include London, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Liverpool, Bristol and other destinations supported by participating operators.
Are baggage rules the same for every operator?
No. Baggage allowances, check-in instructions and ticket conditions vary by operator. Review the exact terms shown before completing the booking.
Should I arrive early for a UK train, coach or ferry?
Follow the arrival and check-in instructions specified by the operator. Ferries, overnight trains and long-distance coaches may require extra time for check-in and finding the correct departure point.
Does GiftCardZoneEU process the booking?
No. GiftCardZoneEU provides independent travel information and affiliate links. Booking, payment, confirmation and customer support are handled by the partner platform or transport operator.
