NordVPN Philippines FAQ

Can I use NordVPN in the Philippines?

Yes. NordVPN applications can be used on supported devices in the Philippines to encrypt internet traffic and improve online privacy.

Does NordVPN protect public Wi-Fi?

A VPN encrypts traffic between your device and the VPN server, which can add useful protection when using shared or unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

Which devices support NordVPN?

NordVPN provides applications for major desktop and mobile operating systems, along with support for selected browsers, smart devices and compatible routers. Available features may vary by platform.

Will a VPN make me completely anonymous?

No. A VPN can mask your visible IP address and encrypt connection traffic, but it does not provide complete anonymity. Account logins, cookies, device information and browsing behavior may still identify you.

Can a VPN replace antivirus software?

No. A VPN and antivirus software serve different purposes. A VPN protects connection traffic, while antivirus tools are designed to detect or block malicious software and files.
