Compare Chiang Mai to Bangkok Train and Bus Tickets

Start with your travel date and compare the departures available. A train seat, sleeper berth and bus seat offer different travel experiences: review the actual category rather than choosing only by price.

Travel option What to compare Before booking
Train seat Seat class, air conditioning or fan category, and departure time. Confirm that the ticket is for a seat rather than a sleeping berth.
Train sleeper Sleeper class, berth position where offered, and ticket collection instructions. Review the berth description and whether your preference can be confirmed.
Bus Seat category, listed facilities, boarding location and Bangkok drop-off point. Check the operator’s baggage allowance and check-in requirements.

Compare the scheduled arrival date as well as the time. When a departure arrives the following day, use that date when arranging accommodation or onward travel.

Choosing a Train Seat or Sleeper Berth

When train tickets are listed for your date, open the details for each departure. Check whether the category offers a seat or a berth, and read the description of the carriage and facilities.

  • Compare the class and cooling type shown for the selected ticket.
  • Check upper or lower berth choices where available.
  • Confirm how passengers travelling together will be allocated places.
  • Read whether the booking requires ticket collection, printing or another step.
  • Check the booking status rather than assuming payment alone confirms your ticket.
For an overnight journey: keep documents, valuables and essential items in a small bag you can access without opening your main luggage.

Comparing Bus Services to Bangkok

Bus departures can differ in seating, facilities and boarding arrangements. Review the operator’s description and listed journey duration for the exact departure you intend to use.

  • Confirm the full boarding address and map pin in Chiang Mai.
  • Check the seating description rather than relying on labels such as “VIP”.
  • Review included luggage and conditions for oversized bags.
  • Check whether meals, stops or onboard facilities are explicitly included.
  • Read the cancellation and refund terms before paying.

Do not assume every operator uses the same terminal. Match the location in your confirmation with the taxi or local transport you arrange.

Check Your Bangkok Arrival Location

A Bangkok destination label is not enough to plan your transfer. Read the full station, terminal or drop-off name on the selected ticket and check its map location against your accommodation.

Continuing to an Airport

If you have a flight after arrival, confirm which airport you need and allow time for delays, collecting luggage, the transfer and airline check-in. Separate tickets may not protect your onward booking if the first journey runs late.

Continuing to Another Destination

Compare your arrival point with the boarding point of your next service. Our Bangkok to Phuket guide and Bangkok to Koh Samui guide can help you review the next leg.

Before arrival: save your hotel address and the full arrival location so you can check your transfer even without a mobile connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I choose a sleeper train or a bus?

Compare your preferred sleeping or seating arrangement, departure time, arrival location and budget. Read the details of the actual ticket category before choosing.

How long does the journey take?

Use the duration listed for your selected departure. Check the arrival date and allow time for possible delays when planning a connection.

Can I choose an upper or lower sleeper berth?

Check whether the selected departure offers berth choices and whether the booking conditions confirm your preference. Availability depends on the service and date.

Where will I arrive in Bangkok?

Use the full arrival location shown on your ticket. Train stations, bus terminals and other drop-off points should not be treated as interchangeable.

Can I bring a large suitcase?

Review the allowance for your selected operator and class. Check extra or oversized luggage conditions before booking.

Can I change or cancel the booking?

Changes, cancellations and refunds depend on the selected ticket. Read its conditions before payment.
