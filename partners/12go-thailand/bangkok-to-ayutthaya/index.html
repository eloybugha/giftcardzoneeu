Choose your transport to Ayutthaya

12Go lists rail and road options on this route. The services available for your date may differ. Compare the full journey, including travel to the Bangkok departure point and onward transport from the Ayutthaya arrival point.

Train

Choose rail if the listed Bangkok station fits your plans. Confirm the departure station, train number and seat class on your ticket.

Check how you will continue from the arrival station to your hotel or sightseeing stop.

Bus or shared van

Compare the exact terminal or meeting point. A shared service may have different pickup arrangements from a station-based departure.

Check luggage limits before paying, especially when carrying a large suitcase.

Private transfer

Consider a private vehicle when you want a pickup that matches your hotel or airport plans.

Confirm the pickup address, destination, passenger capacity, luggage capacity and whether the price is per vehicle.

Departure and arrival points matter

Do not assume every Bangkok departure uses the same station or terminal. Open the listing map and check the address against where you are staying.

For Ayutthaya, check whether your booking ends at a railway station, a van stop or a specific address. Plan the final connection separately unless your ticket explicitly includes it.

Planning a day trip? Check the return journey before confirming your outbound ticket. Allow time to reach the return departure point after sightseeing.

Before you pay

  1. Select Bangkok to Ayutthaya and your travel date.
  2. Compare the listed departure and arrival addresses.
  3. Review the total price, seat or vehicle type, and baggage rules.
  4. Read cancellation, change and ticket collection conditions.
  5. Save your confirmation and follow the operator’s check-in instructions.

A transport ticket does not automatically include a sightseeing tour, temple admission or local transfers. Only rely on inclusions stated in the booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can I travel from Bangkok to Ayutthaya by train?

Yes. Train is one of the transport options on this route. Check the departure station and availability for your date; online listings may not show every rail service.

Should I choose a train or shared van?

Compare the departure address, arrival point, luggage rules and total cost. Choose the service whose full journey best fits your plans.

Can I book a private transfer?

Private transfer listings are available on this route. Confirm your pickup address, destination, vehicle capacity and inclusions before paying.

Can I bring a large suitcase in a shared van?

Rules depend on the operator. Read the baggage allowance and ask for confirmation if your suitcase exceeds it; extra space may require an additional booking.

Does my transport ticket include sightseeing?

Only if the listing explicitly includes it. Check whether temple admission, a guide, waiting time or local transport is part of the booking.
