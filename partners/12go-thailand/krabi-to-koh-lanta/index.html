Krabi Town, Ao Nang or Krabi Airport Pickup?

Do not treat every departure labelled “Krabi” as the same starting point. Use the booking’s address and map pin to confirm where you must meet.

  • Krabi Town: check whether you board at an office, terminal or your accommodation.
  • Ao Nang: confirm the collection area and whether a separate connecting vehicle is included.
  • Krabi Airport: select a service that explicitly offers airport pickup and read the meeting instructions.
  • Railay or another boat-access location: establish how you will reach the listed pickup point before booking a mainland departure.
For hotel collection: provide the full address and check whether the time shown is a pickup window or a terminal departure time.

Shared Vans from Krabi to Koh Lanta

Shared vans are one of the transport options listed for this route. They suit travellers comfortable with a scheduled service and the operator’s stated luggage allowance.

Read the full itinerary

Check whether your booking uses one vehicle or includes a connection. A hotel pickup may be followed by a transfer to another van, so keep your confirmation and bags easy to identify.

  • Confirm the boarding address and reporting time.
  • Check any passenger collections or vehicle changes.
  • Ask about large suitcases, strollers or extra bags.
  • Verify the island crossing arrangements and whether related charges are included.
  • Confirm the exact Koh Lanta drop-off address.
Travelling with bulky luggage? obtain the operator’s approval before paying. Passenger capacity alone does not tell you how much baggage the van can carry.

Road Transfer or Passenger Ferry: Know the Difference

A road itinerary can include a vehicle-ferry crossing as part of the journey. This differs from booking a passenger ferry or speedboat departure from a pier.

Check how each leg works instead of assuming that every ticket mentioning a ferry requires the same boarding process. Follow the operator’s instructions about remaining with your vehicle, changing transport and handling luggage.

  • Identify every road and sea leg in the booking.
  • Check whether you change vehicles or board a boat.
  • Confirm which crossing and transfer fees are included.
  • Keep documents, valuables and essential medication accessible throughout the trip.

Krabi to Koh Lanta by Ferry or Speedboat

Sea services depend on the travel date and conditions. Confirm availability for your actual departure rather than relying on an older timetable.

Compare the departure and arrival piers, any intermediate stops and the land transport needed before and after the sailing.

  • Check whether travel to the departure pier is included.
  • Follow the stated check-in deadline.
  • Review baggage limits and boarding arrangements.
  • Check whether your Koh Lanta hotel transfer is included.
  • Read the cancellation and weather-disruption terms.
Compare door to door: a short sailing still needs time for reaching the pier, checking in, disembarking and getting to your accommodation.

Private Transfers to Koh Lanta

A private transfer may suit a group, a family with children or travellers carrying several bags. Give the provider complete pickup and destination details so the quote matches your actual journey.

  • Confirm whether the price is per vehicle or passenger.
  • Check seats and luggage capacity together.
  • Ask whether crossing fees and hotel delivery are included in the final quote.
  • Request child seats in advance when needed and obtain confirmation.
  • Review waiting-time charges and pickup-change terms.
  • Confirm arrangements for a delayed flight when booking airport collection.
For groups: compare the complete vehicle quote with shared tickets and local transfers for everyone travelling.

Krabi Airport to Koh Lanta After a Flight

Choose an airport-specific pickup where offered. Your aircraft’s arrival time is not the same as the time you will be ready at the meeting point.

  • Allow time for arrival formalities and baggage collection.
  • Provide your flight number when requested.
  • Check the meeting location and operator contact details.
  • Ask what happens if your flight arrives late.
  • Confirm that your selected departure can accommodate your arrival time.

For a late arrival, obtain confirmation of the complete transfer arrangements before booking. Do not assume that an available flight arrival guarantees an onward departure.

Check Your Koh Lanta Arrival Point

A ticket ending at a pier or transport office does not automatically include your accommodation. Match the listed destination with your hotel’s full address.

  • Check whether the booking ends at a pier, office, meeting point or hotel.
  • If hotel delivery is included, verify that your particular address is covered.
  • Ask about any extra charge for accommodation outside the standard drop-off area.
  • Arrange onward transport if your ticket ends before your hotel.
  • Contact your accommodation about access when arriving outside reception hours.
Use the complete hotel address: save its map pin and contact details offline alongside your booking confirmation.

How Long Does Krabi to Koh Lanta Take?

Use the estimate attached to your chosen service. Pickup rounds, traffic, crossing waits, connections and the final hotel location can affect the complete journey.

Compare departure and arrival addresses as well as the advertised duration. Two services with different endpoints may not be directly comparable.

  • Add travel to the boarding point if pickup is excluded.
  • Include the required reporting or check-in time.
  • Read the itinerary for connection waits.
  • Add the final journey to your accommodation.
  • Leave flexibility before a separately booked activity.

Luggage and Total Booking Cost

Compare the final checkout total, then add any transport or baggage costs excluded from the ticket. For a connecting itinerary, review the allowance for every vehicle or boat.

  • Confirm suitcase and hand-luggage limits.
  • Ask about oversized luggage and sporting equipment.
  • Check charges for collection outside the normal area.
  • Verify crossing fees and onward hotel transfers.
  • Read change, cancellation and confirmation conditions.
Before paying: check the passenger count, date, pickup address and destination together. Keep a copy of the selected service’s inclusions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book a van from Krabi to Koh Lanta?

Shared van services are listed for this route. Search your date and check the boarding point, luggage allowance, connections and final drop-off.

Can I travel from Krabi Airport to Koh Lanta?

Look for a service explicitly offering airport pickup. Check the meeting point, your flight arrival time and the provider’s delay policy.

Is Ao Nang hotel pickup included?

Only when the selected service includes your pickup address. Confirm the collection area and whether a connecting vehicle is involved.

Does a road transfer include a ferry crossing?

Road itineraries can include a vehicle-ferry crossing. Check the selected booking for crossing arrangements, charges and any changes of transport.

Will the ticket take me to my Koh Lanta hotel?

Only if your accommodation is included in the stated drop-off area. A booking ending at a pier or office may require separate onward transport.

How much does Krabi to Koh Lanta cost?

Check the final price for your date, passenger count and service. Include any excluded pickup, baggage, crossing or hotel-transfer charges when comparing options.
