Before you book

Pattaya City, North Pattaya, Jomtien and nearby addresses are distinct drop-off areas.

  • Confirm your exact Bangkok pickup address or terminal.
  • Check whether the Pattaya stop is near your accommodation.
  • Review departure time, expected duration and arrival time for your date.
  • Check luggage, passenger count and vehicle size.
  • Read the current cancellation, change and no-show conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I go from Bangkok to Pattaya by bus?

Bus and coach services may be available for your date. Confirm the exact departure and Pattaya arrival points in the current listing.

Can I travel from Bangkok airport directly to Pattaya?

Search from the airport you will actually use: Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK). Availability, meeting points and transfer types vary.

Will a shared van take me to my hotel?

Only if the itinerary explicitly includes that drop-off. Check the listed stop and any transfer you will need afterward.

Is a private transfer priced per person?

Check the listing’s pricing unit and vehicle capacity. Confirm the complete amount and included services before paying.
