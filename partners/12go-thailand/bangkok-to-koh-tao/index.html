Check the mainland connection

Journeys may connect through Chumphon or Surat Thani, depending on the selected service. Confirm the actual itinerary before choosing a ticket.

  • Check the exact mainland arrival station, terminal or airport.
  • Confirm the departure pier and how you get there.
  • Check whether every transfer and boat ticket is included.
  • Review waiting time and both departure and arrival dates.
  • For separate bookings, check what happens if a delay causes a missed connection.

Plan around the boat departure

A mainland arrival does not finish your journey to Koh Tao. Allow time to reach the pier and complete boat check-in.

Boat services can change with operating conditions and weather. Check operator updates before departure and allow flexibility for onward plans.

Before you book

Confirm the complete journey, including your transfer from the Koh Tao arrival pier to your accommodation.

  • Verify the Bangkok departure point and required check-in time.
  • Check overnight dates and the final island arrival time.
  • Confirm seat or berth type where relevant.
  • Review luggage rules for every included transport leg.
  • Check the arrival pier and whether a hotel transfer is included.
  • Read change, cancellation and refund conditions before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Can I travel directly from Bangkok to Koh Tao by bus?

A bus cannot reach the island by road alone. The journey needs a boat crossing. Check whether your ticket includes the bus, pier transfer and ferry.

Can I take a train directly to Koh Tao?

No. A train journey ends on the mainland and needs onward transport to a pier plus a boat crossing to Koh Tao. Verify each connection and which tickets are included.

Can I fly directly to Koh Tao?

Koh Tao has no commercial airport. A flight-based itinerary needs onward ground transport and a boat crossing. Check the full route before booking.

Does my ticket include a transfer to my Koh Tao hotel?

Only if the itinerary explicitly includes your hotel or arrival area. A ferry-pier arrival does not automatically include transport to your accommodation.
