Frequently asked questions

Can I travel from Bangkok to Krabi by bus?

Bus services are an overland option. Check current listings for your date, including the Bangkok terminal and Krabi drop-off point.

Which Bangkok airport serves flights to Krabi?

Check your specific ticket. Bangkok has two separate airports, Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK), so confirm the departure airport before arranging transport.

Is Krabi Airport in Ao Nang?

No. Krabi Airport and Ao Nang are separate locations. Allow time and budget for an onward transfer if you are staying in Ao Nang.

Is a bus or flight better for this route?

Compare the full cost and time to your accommodation. Your best choice depends on the available departures, luggage and preferred arrival time.
