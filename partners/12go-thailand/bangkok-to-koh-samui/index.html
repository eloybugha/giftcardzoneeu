Frequently asked questions

How do I check Bangkok to Koh Samui tickets?

Does a bus ticket include the ferry?

Check the itinerary and included services. Do not assume that a bus-only ticket covers a ferry crossing. Confirm every required leg before completing the booking.

How do I know whether a flight reaches Koh Samui?

Read the destination airport in the flight details. If the itinerary lands elsewhere, check the onward road and ferry connections and whether they are included.

Will my ticket include hotel drop-off?

Only if the selected itinerary explicitly includes hotel drop-off or your destination address. Otherwise, arrange onward transport from the listed airport, pier or stop.

How long does the journey take?

Check the estimated duration in your selected listing. Include time for departure procedures, connections and the journey from the final arrival point to your accommodation.

Can I bring a large suitcase?

Read the luggage allowance for every leg of your itinerary. Check airline baggage charges and any size or weight limits imposed by road or ferry operators.

What should I check for separate connections?

Allow time between services and confirm how you will reach the next departure point. Read each operator’s conditions and check whether a missed connection is covered.

Does GiftCardZoneEU handle my booking?
