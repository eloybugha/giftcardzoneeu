Bangkok to Phuket: bus or flight?

The best fit depends on your travel date, total cost, luggage and where you plan to stay in Phuket.

What matters Bus Flight
Travel time Plan for a long overland trip; compare the listed duration and arrival time. The flight is shorter, but allow time for airport access, check-in and your onward transfer.
Bangkok departure Confirm the specific bus terminal or pickup point in the ticket details. Confirm whether the flight leaves from Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK).
Phuket arrival Check whether the bus stops at a terminal, town or another point, then plan your onward trip. Flights arrive at Phuket Airport (HKT); check the transfer to your hotel or beach area.
Total cost Compare fare, seat type and any transfer from the drop-off point. Include baggage charges and airport transfers when comparing the final cost.

  • Check the exact Phuket arrival point and onward transport to your accommodation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I travel from Bangkok to Phuket by bus?

Yes. Bus options are listed for this route. Check the current departure terminal, drop-off point and schedule for your chosen date on 12Go.

Which Bangkok airport should I use for a flight to Phuket?

Check the ticket carefully: flights may depart from Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK). The airports are separate and require different transfers.

Is the bus or flight better?

Compare the full cost and door-to-door travel time, including baggage and transfers. The better choice depends on your date, budget and destination in Phuket.
