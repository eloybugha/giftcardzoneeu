Krabi Town, Ao Nang or Krabi Airport: Choose the Right Pickup

Krabi Town, Ao Nang and Krabi Airport are different starting points. A ticket labelled “Krabi” does not automatically include collection from all three.

  • Match the listed pickup address or map pin with where you will actually be staying.
  • For hotel pickup, confirm that your full hotel address is inside the included collection area.
  • Check whether the departure time is a pickup window or the vehicle’s scheduled departure from a terminal.
  • For an airport pickup, confirm the meeting point, flight details and waiting-time conditions.
  • If starting from Railay or another boat-access location, confirm how you will reach the stated mainland pickup point.
Send the complete address: a hotel name alone may be unclear. Keep the booking confirmation and pickup instructions available offline.

Shared Vans and Buses from Krabi to Phuket

A shared road service can work well when its boarding and arrival points match your plans. Read the complete itinerary: some bookings involve a vehicle change rather than one uninterrupted ride.

Before booking a shared service

  • Confirm whether you board at a terminal, travel office or your accommodation.
  • Check whether local passenger collections form part of the journey.
  • Read the itinerary for any van-to-van or bus connection.
  • Ask whether you must collect and reload your bags when changing vehicles.
  • Confirm the final drop-off and whether a hotel transfer is included.

Compare luggage rules as carefully as the fare, especially if travelling with large suitcases, a stroller or several bags.

One booking can include several legs: check the vehicle changes and included transfers before paying.

Krabi to Phuket by Ferry

Ferry itineraries may be available for your travel date. Check the actual departure pier and Phuket arrival pier, rather than choosing only by the city names.

Read the full route to see whether it includes land transport, intermediate stops or a boat change. Do not assume every sea itinerary is a direct sailing or includes collection from your hotel.

  • Confirm how you will reach the departure pier and the required check-in time.
  • Check whether pickup and onward land transfers are included in the quoted fare.
  • Review baggage limits and handling arrangements.
  • Read the operator’s conditions for changes, cancellations and weather disruption.
  • Keep valuables, travel documents and electronics protected from water.
Planning a flight after the ferry? allow time for disembarkation, your transfer to Phuket Airport and airline check-in. Avoid relying on a tight connection.

Private Transfers from Krabi to Phuket

A private vehicle may suit a family or group wanting an agreed pickup and destination. Confirm the full route with the provider, including your hotel, airport or other requested address.

  • Provide the complete pickup and destination addresses.
  • Check seating and luggage capacity together.
  • Confirm whether the price is per vehicle or passenger.
  • Review fuel, tolls, parking and any other listed charges.
  • Request child seats in advance when needed and confirm availability.
  • Read waiting-time, cancellation and pickup-change conditions.

For a group, compare the full vehicle quote with the combined cost of shared tickets and local transfers for everyone travelling.

Where Will You Arrive in Phuket?

A Phuket terminal, pier or office is not automatically close to your accommodation. Patong, Kata, Karon, Phuket Town and Phuket Airport are different destinations.

  • Check the exact arrival address and map pin.
  • Confirm whether the booking ends at a bus terminal, pier, office, airport or hotel.
  • If a hotel transfer is included, verify that it covers your specific address.
  • Budget for onward transport when the ticket ends before your final destination.
  • Check accommodation access if arriving before check-in or late in the evening.
Flying from Phuket? search for an airport drop-off where offered, or plan a separate transfer from the stated arrival point. A ticket to “Phuket” does not automatically end at the airport.

How Long Does Krabi to Phuket Take?

Use the duration shown for your selected departure. Road traffic, passenger collections, stops, boat connections and your final destination can change the total journey time.

Compare the complete trip rather than only the main ride or sailing. A ferry itinerary also needs time for getting to the pier, check-in, disembarkation and onward travel.

  • Add travel from your accommodation to the meeting point.
  • Follow the operator’s required reporting time.
  • Include any transfer waits listed in the itinerary.
  • Leave a buffer before flights, tours or separately booked connections.
Scheduled arrival is an estimate: choose a departure that leaves room for delays when you have a fixed onward commitment.

Luggage and Booking Conditions

Check the allowance for every vehicle or boat in your itinerary. A connecting van may have different luggage limits from the main bus or ferry.

  • Confirm suitcase and hand-luggage allowances.
  • Ask about oversized bags, strollers and sporting equipment before paying.
  • Check whether extra luggage requires approval, an additional fee or another seat.
  • Review the final total, including listed pickup and onward-transfer charges.
  • Read cancellation, change and confirmation conditions.

Keep identification, valuables, medication and your booking confirmation in a small bag you can access throughout the journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I travel from Krabi to Phuket by road?

Road options include shared vans, buses or connections and private transfers, depending on your date and starting point. Check the selected itinerary for the exact service.

Can I take a ferry from Krabi to Phuket?

Ferry itineraries may be available for your date. Confirm the piers, included land transfers, intermediate stops and any boat changes before booking.

Is Ao Nang pickup included in a Krabi ticket?

Only if the selected service explicitly includes your Ao Nang pickup location. Check the collection area and provide your full accommodation address.

Does the ticket include a transfer to Patong?

Only when Patong or your specific hotel is included in the booking. Otherwise, arrange onward travel from the stated Phuket arrival point.

Can I book a transfer to Phuket Airport?

Search for airport-specific drop-offs where offered and confirm that your booking ends at Phuket Airport. Allow extra time for delays and airline check-in.

How much does Krabi to Phuket cost?

Check the current final price for your travel date, passenger count and pickup area. Include local transfers and listed baggage charges when comparing the total cost.
