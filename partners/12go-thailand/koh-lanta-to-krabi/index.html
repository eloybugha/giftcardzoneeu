Understand the Road and Ferry Crossing

A road transfer can include a vehicle-ferry crossing as part of the itinerary. That differs from a passenger ferry or speedboat ticket departing from a pier.

Read the journey details to understand whether you remain with the same vehicle, change transport or board a separate boat. Follow the operator’s instructions at each stage.

  • Identify the road and sea sections of your booking.
  • Check any connections or vehicle changes.
  • Confirm included crossing and transfer fees.
  • Keep documents, valuables and essential medication accessible during the journey.
