Booking a Separate Connection

Avoid a tight connection based only on the scheduled arrival time. If you book separate tickets, check the conditions of each booking and allow room for delays.

Seat Options and Luggage Conditions

Read the description of the service you intend to book. Seat labels alone do not confirm the exact legroom, onboard facilities or baggage allowance.

  • Check the seat category and any available seating diagram.
  • Review the listed onboard amenities.
  • Check baggage limits before travelling with large suitcases.
  • Ask about oversized items if the booking details do not explain them.
  • Keep valuables, documents and essential items in your hand luggage.
