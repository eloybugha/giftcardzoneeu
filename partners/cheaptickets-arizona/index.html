Why visit Arizona?

Desert cities, red rocks and unforgettable road trips

Arizona combines vibrant desert cities, red-rock landscapes, Native American heritage, national parks and scenic road trips across one of the most visually distinctive destinations in the United States.

  • Explore Phoenix museums, dining, sports and desert attractions.
  • Enjoy Scottsdale resorts, shopping, galleries and nightlife.
  • Experience Sedona red rocks, hiking trails and scenic viewpoints.
  • Visit Tucson for Sonoran cuisine, history and outdoor adventures.
  • Combine a city stay with Flagstaff, Route 66 or the Grand Canyon.

Where to stay

Popular Arizona areas for different trips

Choose a destination based on your plans for sightseeing, outdoor activities, dining, nightlife, business travel or airport access.

Phoenix

Ideal for museums, dining, professional sports, desert gardens and convenient PHX Airport connections.

Scottsdale

Popular for desert resorts, golf, galleries, restaurants, shopping, wellness trips and lively entertainment districts.

Sedona

Popular for red-rock scenery, hiking, wellness retreats, scenic drives, galleries and memorable outdoor experiences.

Tucson

Known for Sonoran Desert landscapes, distinctive food, historic districts, museums and Saguaro National Park.

Flagstaff & Grand Canyon

Great for Route 66 history, cooler mountain scenery, outdoor adventures and trips to Grand Canyon National Park.

PHX Airport Area

Practical for early departures, late arrivals, short stopovers and quick access to Phoenix, Tempe and Scottsdale.

Arizona travel planning

Useful details before booking

PHX and TUS airports

PHX serves the Phoenix metropolitan area, while TUS is the main gateway for Tucson. Both offer taxi, rideshare and car-rental connections.

Desert and national parks

Compare seasonal hotel demand around major Arizona cities, Sedona, the Grand Canyon, Saguaro National Park and desert resorts.

Sports and events

Hotel demand can rise during major games, concerts, conventions, festivals and university events.

Getting around

Local transit varies by destination. Rideshare services are common in major cities, while a rental car can be useful for national parks and multi-city road trips.

Flexible dates

Adjusting travel dates, flight times or hotel areas may reveal different prices and booking conditions.

Booking protection

Save confirmation emails and review change or cancellation rules for every part of your reservation.

Frequently asked questions

CheapTickets Arizona booking FAQ

Can I compare Arizona flights and hotels with CheapTickets?

Yes. CheapTickets lets travelers search flights, hotels, vacation packages and car rentals for Arizona.

Which Arizona area is best for first-time visitors?

Phoenix suits city attractions and sports, while Scottsdale offers resorts, golf and nightlife. Choose Sedona for red-rock scenery, Tucson for Sonoran culture, or Flagstaff and the Grand Canyon for a wider road trip.

Which airport should I select for Arizona?

Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport uses the code PHX and is the state's main gateway. Tucson International Airport uses TUS, while Flagstaff Pulliam Airport, code FLG, can be useful for selected northern Arizona itineraries.

Can a flight and hotel package be cheaper?

A package may provide competitive value for some dates. Compare the package total with separate flight and hotel prices.

When should I book an Arizona hotel?

Booking earlier may provide more choices during school holidays, summer, major sporting events, festivals, conventions, spring training and holiday periods.

Who handles my reservation after booking?

CheapTickets or the provider shown in your confirmation handles payments, changes, cancellations and customer service.
