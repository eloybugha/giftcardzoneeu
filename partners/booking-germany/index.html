Why Germany?

Historic cities, modern hubs and scenic regions

Germany offers major cultural capitals, business centers, medieval towns, mountain escapes and easy city-to-city rail connections.

  • Explore Berlin’s landmarks, museums and diverse neighborhoods.
  • Stay in Munich for Bavarian culture and access to southern Germany.
  • Discover Hamburg’s harbor, canals and entertainment districts.
  • Use Frankfurt as a business, airport or rail-travel base.
  • Combine Cologne, Düsseldorf and Rhine-area destinations.

Useful checks

Details that can change the quality of your stay

Station access

A hotel near local transit can reduce walking and make sightseeing easier in large cities.

Breakfast

Check whether breakfast is included in the selected room rate or available for an added charge.

Parking

City-center parking may be limited or paid. Confirm availability before arriving by car.

Check-in hours

Late arrivals may require advance notice, especially at smaller hotels and apartments.

Major events

Trade fairs, festivals, football matches and holidays can increase demand and room prices.

Cancellation rules

Flexible and non-refundable rates can have very different prices and conditions.
