Can NordVPN protect connections on public Wi-Fi?

A VPN creates an encrypted connection between your device and the VPN server. This can help reduce exposure when using unsecured or shared Wi-Fi, although users should still avoid suspicious links and unsafe websites.

Does NordVPN work on phones and computers?

NordVPN provides applications for common operating systems including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. Compatibility can vary by device and subscription.

Will a VPN make me completely anonymous?

No service can guarantee complete anonymity. A VPN improves privacy by encrypting traffic and masking your public IP address, but good password, browser and account-security practices remain important.

Can a VPN affect internet speed?

VPN encryption and server routing may affect speed. Results depend on your original internet connection, server distance, network congestion, selected protocol and device performance.
