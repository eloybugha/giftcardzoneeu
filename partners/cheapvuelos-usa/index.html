Flight booking FAQ

Cheapvuelos USA questions

What can I compare through Cheapvuelos?

You can review available flight itineraries, cabin options, schedules and displayed prices for your selected route and dates. Availability changes according to the search details.

Which cabin classes may appear?

Results may include economy, premium economy, business or first class, depending on the airline, aircraft, route and travel date.

Is the first displayed fare the final price?

Not always. Baggage, seat selection, service charges and other optional items can affect the total. Check the final checkout amount before paying.

Who handles my reservation and support?

Cheapvuelos or the travel provider identified during checkout handles reservations, payments, ticket changes, cancellations and customer support. GiftCardZoneEU does not process bookings.

Can I compare another flight site?

Yes. You can also open our CheapTickets affiliate option to compare availability and booking conditions before deciding.
