Check the Offer Before You Book

When a promotion is listed above, read its full conditions before choosing a ticket. A displayed offer does not confirm that it is available for your account or selected journey.

  • Eligibility: check whether the offer applies to your account.
  • Route restrictions: confirm that your selected journey qualifies.
  • Expiry and limits: review the deadline and any redemption quota.
  • Discount cap: check the maximum saving and eligible amount.
  • Checkout: confirm the accepted discount and final total.
If no offer is displayed: you can still compare tickets using the normal booking buttons. Do not assume a discount is available.

How to Check a Japan Booking on 12Go

  1. Open 12Go: use a booking button on this page.
  2. Search your journey: enter your departure point, destination and travel date.
  3. Compare the available products: review the transport actually listed for your search.
  4. Read the selected ticket details: check locations, luggage and booking conditions.
  5. Apply a code if eligible: enter it where a promo-code field is provided.
  6. Review the final total: confirm any discount before paying.
The booking link and promo code are separate: opening a link does not guarantee that a code has been applied automatically.

Check These Details Before Paying

  • Passenger information: enter names and other details as required.
  • Travel date: check departure and arrival dates, especially for overnight journeys.
  • Exact locations: locate the named station, stop or pickup point.
  • Ticket delivery: check whether you receive a boarding ticket, collection instructions or a voucher to exchange.
  • Luggage: read the selected service's allowance and conditions.
  • Connections: allow time between separately booked journeys.
  • Changes and cancellations: review the terms of the selected product.
  • Final price: check the complete total, currency and any accepted discount.

What If a Promo Code Does Not Work?

If a code is rejected or no discount appears, check the conditions before continuing.

  • Check the spelling and remove extra spaces.
  • Review account eligibility and route restrictions.
  • Check the stated expiry and redemption limits.
  • Read any explanation displayed at checkout.
  • Contact 12Go support for booking-specific questions.
Review the price before confirming: if a discount is not applied, decide whether the displayed total still suits your plans.

Japan Promo & Booking FAQ

Where can I check Japan promotion information?

When an offer is listed on this page, read its terms and check your eligibility. Confirm acceptance and the actual discount on 12Go before paying. If no offer is displayed, you can still search for tickets.

Does the booking link automatically apply a discount?

Do not assume a discount is applied automatically. If you have an eligible code, enter it where a promo-code field is provided and confirm the final total before paying.

Are all Japan transport products available on 12Go?

Search your exact route and date to see the products listed for your journey. Do not assume that every operator, transport type or departure is available.

What should I do if a promo code is rejected?

Check the spelling and the offer's eligibility, expiry, route restrictions and redemption limits. Review any explanation shown by 12Go and decide whether the final price still suits your plans.
